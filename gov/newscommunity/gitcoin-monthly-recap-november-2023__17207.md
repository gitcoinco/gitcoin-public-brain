We’re back with our monthly update for all things Gitcoin: Gitcoin Grants, Grants Stack, Gitcoin Passport and Allo.

![|624x44](upload://zmDMvTNA5BTBsXywEkaGkcJVxK8.png)
GG19 ended this week, and we are very happy to report that we hit our goal of $600k donated! Thank you to everyone who participated in Funding What Matters to them in this incredible final round of 2023.

*What's next?* The team will conduct sybil analysis, with payouts aimed to go out before the end of the year.

Our[ archive.grants.gitcoin.co](http://archive.grants.gitcoin.co/) is now live!

![|624x44](upload://4VbZHt2299Y4ti1FVkGvtXLSTru.png)
The Allo team has been hard at work in November, deploying v2 and beginning to build applications on top of the new protocol. Some highlights:

* Allo v2 is now on mainnets!! We’ve deployed to Ethereum Mainnet, Optimism, Arbitrum, Base, and PGN. Our contract addresses are [here](https://github.com/allo-protocol/allo-v2/tree/main/contracts) and our docs are [here](https://docs.allo.gitcoin.co/overview).
* We’re excited to introduce [SeaGrants](https://grants-app-smoky.vercel.app/), a new app that enables communities to designate small groups of voters to approve small grants. This was built entirely using the [Allo v2 SDK](https://github.com/allo-protocol/allo-v2-sdk) — we’ve been iterating on this SDK so that we can enable builders to spin up new concepts as quickly as possible. You can learn more about the SeaGrants app [here](https://hackmd.io/@l_tr1sZIRF6Sulx08CdXlQ/B1XzfzR4a).
* Arbitrum Grants DAO is [sponsoring a hackathon for Allo on Arbitrum](https://app.buidlbox.io/arbitrum/allo-on-arbitrum). Check it out!
* This month we’ve also been scoping a number of protocol integrations — we’re excited to share more in the near future!

![|624x44](upload://eaUPndRBVSbdoJLQX22PsKNVhmp.png)
It’s been a busy month at Grants Stack, from supporting GG19, UX updates and a QV activation at Schelling Point IST.

* **The American Cancer Society** is running an independent round! View the incredible projects and [donate to this round here](https://www.gitcoin.co/blog/american-cancer-society-project-showcase)! (Note: Gitcoin Passport is not required for matching for this round).
* We’ve been making improvements to Grant Stack’s UX, including a new [Explorer](https://explorer.gitcoin.co/) homepage and round edit flow
* Rounds Currently Running on Grants Stack
  * Polygon
  * PowerPool
  * SpaceID
  * Uniswap-Arbitrum Grant Program
  * GMX
  * The American Cancer Society
* [Grants Stack Retroactive PGF](https://www.gitcoin.co/grants-stack/rpgf) page now live
* We ran a mini QV round at Schelling Point IST to distribute 1 ETH between 4 Turkish charities. View the round’s report card [here](https://reportcards.gitcoin.co/10/0x8916763E325C5aB2BfEc4e8dd06423Ca9d28c373)
* Check out how Arbitrum leveraged QF to gather community insights and boost engagement within their ecosystem in our latest case study:[ Arbitrum Case Study ](https://www.gitcoin.co/blog/arbitrum-case-study)
* [Grants Stack Release Notes](https://www.notion.so/gitcoin/What-s-New-Gitcoin-Grants-Stack-8495cce451bf4bc6881539746902e7c5)
* If you’re interested in running your own round, please [schedule a demo](https://z13p2cf7n7b.typeform.com/to/ZR5fKsjy?utm_campaign=Grants+Stack&utm_medium=email&utm_source=hs_email&typeform-source=21870089.hubspotpreview-na1.com)

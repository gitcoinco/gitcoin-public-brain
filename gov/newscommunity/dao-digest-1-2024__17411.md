**Project Updates**

* COMPLETED:
  * Announcing: [The Village Infra on Polygon#1 Round on Grants Stack](https://www.gitcoin.co/blog/village-infra-on-polygon-round-1-on-grants-stack)
  * 44/50 rounds run–Any mechanism, inclusive of community/self-serve/serviced
  * 7/10 repeat rounds run–Excludes Gitcoin-affiliated operators. Proving value generation for grants managers

* IN PROGRESS:
  * Wrapping up Base & zkSync deployment → Lit Protocol dependency
  * Resolving Polygon issues
  * Scroll deployment
  * Starting work on Allo v2 Migration

* ON THE HORIZON: Complete and stabilize community donation experience
  * Stabilize and improve new Direct Grants feature
  * Connect Grants Programs with best builders for their needs
  * Scope and kick off next-gen architecture, including Allo v2

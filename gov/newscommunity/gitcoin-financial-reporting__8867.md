schlabach | 2022-05-20 15:06:07 UTC | #12

Hi @umarkhaneth - please see my comment above regarding the timing of the treasury diversification, as well as [this thread](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/treasury-diversification-working-group-updates-thread/10231) for more details. Unfortunately it's not quite as easy as simply swapping GTC into DAI or USDC; while Gitcoin DAO might have been able to get some stables by doing so, there is not enough liquidity to do so on-chain in a timely fashion. Had we swapped GTC on the open market, we would have experienced material slippage and a negative price impact on GTC. Further, selling GTC in the open market would have resulted in the token ending up in the hands of short-term holders; the benefit of working with institutional partners is that we can select partners who are aligned and long-term oriented.

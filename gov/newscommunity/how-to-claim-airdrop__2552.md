The FAQ for GTC and the airdrop can be found here http://support.gitcoin.co/kb/section/4 **Please check the FAQ before posting your question!** If the dApp shows you have *0 GTC* to claim, it may be because, as of the Gitcoin token launch on May 25, 2021, **GTC was allocated to people who participated in Gitcoin grants, hackathons, bounties, or KERNEL prior to April 1, 2021.** Please review the eligibility requirements and follow the instructions to complete and submit the form if you feel you should be eligible: https://support.gitcoin.co/kb/article/32-how-do-i-know-if-i-m-eligible-for-the-airdrop/ For eligible participants, there is a 30 day period to claim your GTC.(editado)

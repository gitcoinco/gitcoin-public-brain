# Messari Governor DAO Profile Update for Gitcoin

<https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/messari-governor-dao-profile-update-for-gitcoin/12976>
joel.miya | 2023-02-17 14:52:04 UTC | #1

gm Gitcoin!

My name is [Joel](https://twitter.com/Ichiro_JJ) and I’m a governance analyst at [Messari](https://messari.io/governor/overview). We’re happy to announce that we recently upgraded our DAO profiles on Governor!

Aside from the coverage of proposals (for free users), we now provide rich on-chain and off-chain analytics for [Gitcoin](https://messari.io/dao/gitcoin-governance) for Pro & Enterprise users.

This includes:

* Delegate metrics: voting power breakdown, their voting power growth over time, and recent voting activity

![|624x407](upload://jg2HOH9jb3iCa29pR8pPCPPPxmz.jpeg)

* DAO community engagement metrics: lifetime voters and average voter participation rate

![|624x315](upload://jcl7MdWcMnVrwwclWEAS55tSzIf.jpeg)

* Proposal metrics: lifetime author counts, counts of proposals in a given timeframe as well as average proposal pass rate

![|624x299](upload://mav7favZ7CO1QRDk0H1lBaEpxUI.jpeg)

Any feedback is welcomed and we would be happy to chat about other services we can provide (ex: [quarterly reports](https://messari.io/report/state-of-uniswap-q4-2022?referrer=all-research), governance [analysis](https://messari.io/report/state-of-optimism-governance?referrer=grid-view) and [overview](https://messari.io/report/state-of-synthetix-governance?referrer=all-research)).

Hoping the community finds these metrics valuable :slight_smile:

Thanks for the feedback Kieran!

Agreed that a lot of interesting discussions happen on the forums, especially Gitcoin's forum. The Governor team does our best to provide a TLDR summary for each proposal. The goal is generally less than 300 words.

The reason we don't cover all the recent forum posts is because our scope is to only track governance discussions/proposals that will go to an off-chain or on-chain vote.

However, we have an aggregated view of all Gitcoin proposals that you can see [here](https://messari.io/dao/gitcoin-governance/proposals).

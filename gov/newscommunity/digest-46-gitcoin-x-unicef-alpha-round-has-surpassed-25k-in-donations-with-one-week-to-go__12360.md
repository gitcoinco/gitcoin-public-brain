## Unicef Alpha Round–Results to Date 📊

Our alpha grants round with Unicef is in full swing! As of 12/14, we have over 1000 unique donators. The round has also [surpassed $25k USD in donations](https://dune.com/cerv1/gitcoin-unicef), from combined donations in both ETH and DAI.

You can follow the progress of the round via [this Dune dashboard](https://dune.com/cerv1/gitcoin-unicef), created by Gitcoin Steward, [Carl Cervone](https://twitter.com/carl_cervone), to track donations and other relevant statistics. Thank you Carl!

**Some additional Unicef updates:**

* MMM’s own Armando created this [90-second announcement video](https://twitter.com/gitcoin/status/1602317889931214849?s=20&t=USwJKA0Bn1AQIkXqGIWivA) about the round. If you’re looking to spread the word about this grants round, this video is a great way to do it. [Share it with your friends!](https://twitter.com/gitcoin/status/1602317889931214849?s=20&t=USwJKA0Bn1AQIkXqGIWivA)
* [Ben West](https://twitter.com/BenWest) and [Azeem](https://twitter.com/azeemk_) ran our [Twitter Space with Unicef](https://twitter.com/i/spaces/1OyKAVkpNlbGb?s=20) last Tuesday, 12/13. You can [listen to the recording here.](https://twitter.com/i/spaces/1OyKAVkpNlbGb?s=20)
* [@web3beach](https://twitter.com/web3beach) made [this fantastic tutorial video](https://twitter.com/gitcoin/status/1602291947036676096?s=20&t=USwJKA0Bn1AQIkXqGIWivA) on how to donate to the round from your phone.

There’s still one week to go. Donate today and your donation will be quadratically-matched from the 50 ETH matching pool.

[**Donate here.**](https://grant-explorer.gitcoin.co/#/round/1/0xdf75054cd67217aee44b4f9e4ebc651c00330938)

### ☑️ Top Posts From the Gov. Forum

*These are the top posts from this week in the Gitcoin Governance forum. [See the latest here](https://gov.gitcoin.co/latest).*

* [**Gitcoin updates summarized**](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gitcoin-updates-summarized/12337) **by** [**Kyle Weiss**
](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/kyle)
In this post, [Kyle](https://twitter.com/kbw) gives a short “state of the DAO” post. Updates include: recently [electing a new Steward council](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/v2-of-the-steward-council-nomination-thread-and-election-update/12057), Gitcoin recently closed its [treasury diversification efforts](https://go.gitcoin.co/blog/gitcoin-expands-governance-participation-to-long-term-partners), our Grants Protocols is live(!) and we’re running two Alpha Rounds on it currently. Also, Gitcoin Passport continues to grow–we recently announced a [partnership with Snapshot](https://snapshot.mirror.xyz/wFAGLoy1EKRFr34wQ8lYdrdcCoENeyrgjyEGl_DPn00), and Passport has recently passed over 100k passports created.

* [**Anti-Sybil Legos**](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/anti-sybil-legos/12265) **by** [**j-cook**
](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/j-cook)

  This post gives a high-level outline of the way FDD is thinking about composable anti-Sybil legos, as Gitcoin moves to the protocol-based grants system. Essentially, Gitcoin depends on Gitcoin Grants, and Gitcoin Grants depends on effective sybil defense. Right now, there are human-powered elements to sybil defense, such as human reviews and using data science tools. In the future, FDD aims to create modular “anti-sybil legos”, that “can also be forked and recombined in creative ways in other projects, making them useful across the whole of Web3, not just within Gitcoin”.

* [**November CSDO Digest**](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/november-csdo-digest/12338) **by** [**Kyle Jensen**
](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/kylejensen)
Cross-Stream DAO Operations (CSDO) is a group of workstream and initiative leads that come together to ensure alignment, transparency, and integrity across the DAO. In this post, Kyle introduces the monthly CSDO Digest, and breaks down all the most important CSDO outcomes from every month, starting with September.

Read more about the work CSDO is doing with [pods](https://www.notion.so/gitcoin/Pods-b2f019d9c5fa4944a9782b02cd511585), auditing [workstream budgets](https://www.notion.so/gitcoin/Pods-b2f019d9c5fa4944a9782b02cd511585), and the initiative to create [a DAO operating system](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/november-csdo-digest/12338#key-discussions-thoughts-5).

## 🌱 This Week’s Content

*This week, two new posts on the Gitcoin blog, and three new Green Pill episodes from Owocki!*

* **Gitcoin Blog:** [**The power of participating in hackathons**
](https://go.gitcoin.co/blog/the-power-of-participating-in-hackathons)
Quinn dispels popular myths about participating in hackathons(such as “hackathons are only for developers”). They also explore the benefits of participating in hackathons, such as: gaining experience on technical teams, creating useful products, and landing a job. The post also offers useful tips for those considering participating in their first hackathon.

* **Gitcoin Blog:** [**Successful grants programs in web3 history**
](https://go.gitcoin.co/blog/successful-grants-programs-in-web3-history)
This post is a case-study, sharing best practices from three successful web3 grants programs: Uniswap Grants Program, Aave Grants DAO, and ETH Ecosystem Support Program. It examines some of the difficulties each grants program has faced, and how they created solutions to overcome those difficulties.

* **Greenpill #70:** [**Circles UBI with Martin Koppelmann**
](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uFGKoV2knYI)Today, we’re speaking with [Martin Koppelmann](https://twitter.com/koeppelmann) of [Gnosis](https://twitter.com/gnosisSafe) and [Circles UBI](https://twitter.com/CirclesUBI). Circles UBI is a web-of-trust mechanism for digital identity, which is a necessary primitive for Universal Basic Income.

* **Greenpill #71:** [**Decentralized Identity with Gregory Rocco of Spruce**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BKUrBr815zw)

  Kevin speaks with [Gregory Rocco](https://twitter.com/Obstropolos) of [Spruce](https://twitter.com/SpruceID), which lets users control their data across the web. Gregory is also a massive proponent of sign-in with Ethereum, decentralized identity, reputation, and society.

* **Greenpill #72:** [**Digital Identity is Not Human Identity with Phillip Sheldrake**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wcCenM5A-l8)

  Greenpill welcomes [Philip Sheldrake](https://twitter.com/Sheldrake). Philip is a writer, thinker, educator, and moral philosopher. Philip and Kevin critique the current web3 identity dynamic and how it’s flawed compared to how we actually act in the world. Philip also gives some valuable advice to builders towards the end of the episode.

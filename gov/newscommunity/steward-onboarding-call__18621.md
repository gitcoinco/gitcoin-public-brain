## GM Gitcoin Stewards!

We are resuming regular Steward onboarding calls for new Stewards.

## Who is this for?

1. Anyone who is interested in being a Steward at Gitcoin.
2. Someone who has already signed up and either feels lost or wants to better understand how they can contribute.

## What to expect?

You’ll receive a 30 min introduction to Gitcoin, its structure, governance mechanics and details on how to get started. You’ll also have the opportunity to ask any questions at the end of the call.

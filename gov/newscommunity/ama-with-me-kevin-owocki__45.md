owocki | 2021-05-28 01:34:32 UTC | #8

hi @zkwaffles thanks for stopping by.

> curious about your vision for gitcoindao… 

basically the idea is for gitcoindao to be a schelling point for the hopeful; for those who want to build a vessel that can go to the quadratic lands - the promised land of digital democracy and public goods being funded.  

tangibly that means creating a DAO of DAOs - a generator function (a function that generates other functions) that generates projects/DAOs/companies that fund public goods.

this is of course new framing of what we've been doing for a very long time => gitcoin.co/results . coordinating builders & funders to build/fund the open web.

> more specifically what might the future look like for less experienced (not quite steward level) contributors? working groups? forum discussions and proposals? I’m sure there are a lot of people wanting to pitch in down the road
the workstreams that i've seen proposed from the community

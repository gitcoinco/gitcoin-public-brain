# 🔥 Hot Topics

![|690x388](upload://tlD8JNEI8r4YYkKDAOet5dLFOtK.png)

🚀 Gitcoin is launching its [Citizen Grants program](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/proposal-gitcoin-s-citizen-grants-program/17470), split into Citizens Retro (Retroactive Funding), Citizens Innovate (Citizen Proposals) and Citizens Forward (RFPs), to continue fostering innovation and [community participation](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gitcoin-citizens-what-are-you-doing/16922).

🧠 Grantee discovery remains an unsolved problem and @rohit is proposing prototypes for solving it. Learn more about his early results and provide feedback for [Personalized Grantee Recommendations](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/concepts-pocs-and-early-results-for-personalized-grantee-recommendations/17469).

🤝 DeveloperDAO has allocated 500k CODE tokens to Gitcoin in line with our partnership agreement. These tokens are currently held in the Safe, playing a key role in [uniting our communities](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/update-gitcoin-and-developerdao-partnership-in-2024/17423) and leveraging our combined strengths to support the growth of the web3 space.

🏃‍♂️ Can we create more value for Gitcoin by Rapid Prototyping in highest value areas? @owocki proposes [a new workstream](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/temp-check-rapid-prototyping-gitcoin/17419) to create alignment among stakeholders that can act as a 0-1 booster/assist for 0-10 product workstreams.

🆕 Grants Managers can create and run rounds on either **Base** or **zkSync Era** via [Grants Stack Manager](http://manager.gitcoin.co)! and similarly, any builders can also choose to create a project profile on those chains via [Grants Stack Builder](https://builder.gitcoin.co/).

🔚 PGN will be sunset [(lights out) in June of 2024](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/announcing-the-spin-down-of-pgn-eol-june-2024/17430). In the meantime, the plan is to disable bridging onto PGN, and users are encouraged to bridge assets off PGN as they need them.

📊 Explore [Allo Protocol multichain metrics and trends](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/allo-protocol-multichain-metrics-and-trends/17475) with data sourced from Arbitrum, Avalanche, Base, Ethereum, Fantom, Optimism, Polygon, and zkSync.

🌏 GCC, in collaboration with Mask Network and Metapool, orchestrated a symphony of support, amplifying the impact with an estimated $19,710 in matching funds. Learn more about Gitcoin’s [Global Chinese Community](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/recap-of-global-chinese-community-gcc-round-and-our-plan-for-gg20/17486).

🟢 It’s Impact Season! Get up to speed with the [latest season](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qxvFT0t_qPw&list=PLF5CNzrKS9sr_wPF2YK0QwSwtWHej6Ifc) of the Green Pill Podcast.

🔴 Meet [EasyRetroPGF.xyz](https://x.com/owocki/status/1745845124913914028?s=20) - a new tool to allow anyone to do Opimtism style RPGF.

🎓 [Student Blockchain Club GG20 Rounds](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/student-blockchain-club-gg20-rounds-announcement-call-for-sponsorship/17441): @sejalrekhan presents an initiative to expand the reach and understanding of crypto and its underlying mechanisms to a broader audience.

🤓 Calling all intellectually curious theorists, program designers, and data scientists to contribute to the [RegenLearnings.xyz](https://regenlearnings.xyz/) research group started by @owocki and @ccerv1.

🌟 Read ‘[A North Star for Gitcoin (and the decade-long path to get there)](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/a-north-star-for-gitcoin/17265)’ to learn more about our current course towards the north star, and how your work can support reaching it.

## 🗳️ **GitcoinDAO Governance Overview:**

* Snapshot polls GTC Stewards. Tally performs on-chain actions.
* Proposals that have on-chain steps, such as budget requests, are approved on Snapshot before moving on to Tally.

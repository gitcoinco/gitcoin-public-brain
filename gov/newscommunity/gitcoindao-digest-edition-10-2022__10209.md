## [🤖 Meta-Governance](https://gov.gitcoin.co/c/governance-meta/6)

* [Grants Policy Update: Projects with Tokens](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/proposal-grants-policy-update-projects-with-tokens/10125) by [Annika](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/annika) is intended to “provide context on, and guide the community to a decision around, grant eligibility criteria as it pertains to projects with tokens.” The post is a follow-up & summary of the existing discussions outlined in[ Novel Situation #1](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/novel-situation-1-project-should-not-have-a-token-or-raised-vc-funding/10029), and[ Proposed Revision to Grant Eligibility Criteria 1](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/proposed-revision-to-grant-eligibility-criteria/10098).

* [Partnership & Mutual Grant with Wonder](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/proposal-partnership-mutual-grant-with-wonder/10170) by [Andros](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/andros) is a proposal that “if passed, would issue 100,000 GTC for governance rights equating to 2,500,000 $WONDER tokens (2.5% of total supply) when Wonder’s token is live.”

* [Decentralizing Public Speaking about Gitcoin](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/decentralizing-public-speaking-about-gitcoin/10100) by [Owocki](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/owocki) is a call for DAO contributors to join a working group that will diversify the voices that are public speaking about Gitcoin, our mission, impact, philosophy, etc.

* [Minimum Viable Product](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/minimum-viable-product/10139) by [Owocki](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/owocki) is an invitation to apply Web 2 product philosophy to Web 3 by building, designing, and testing usable, reliable, and functional products.

* [Minimum Shippable Increments](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/minimum-shippable-increments/10140) by [Owocki](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/owocki) expands on the idea of the MVP with open source software principles to discuss how products can/should be built in public!

* [Its time to level up our emails](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/its-time-to-level-up-our-emails/10134) by [jahnsson](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/jahnsson/summary) reflects on the tools we’ve been using to manage our email campaigns and suggests a path forward to improve our data insights, growth, and impact.

* [Treasury Diversification Working Group Update](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/treasury-diversification-working-group-update-3-19-2022/10128) by [schlabach](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/schlabach) provided [an update](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/treasury-diversification-working-group-update-3-19-2022/10128) on the Treasury Diversification Working Group. If you’d like to recommend a strategic partner, please leave a comment on the [second forum post](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/treasury-diversification-part-ii-welcoming-strategic-partners/9825) or contact a member of the working group.

* [Ether’s Phoenix Game [Version 0]](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/ethers-phoenix-game-version-0/10113) by [Colton](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/colton/summary) introduces Ether’s Phoenix (EP) -- an exciting gamification of the process of supporting public goods. Your actions shape a constantly evolving PFP and increase (or decrease) your Regen Score. EP proliferates coordination lore, unifies impactDAOs through a shared game experience, and provides a trackable metric of greenpilled people (Regen Score).

* [Elliot](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/Elliott) published an [update](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gitcoin-financial-reporting/8867/5) from Llama on [Gitcoin Financial Reporting for February 2022](https://doc-0o-6s-docs.googleusercontent.com/docs/securesc/5qb3ou4l183q2oc9iqghlnga2st6uuvi/vutpst17m0qjkkp3ifb8d853k6ohktag/1648046550000/07219962111518817433/11838597598179151713/1PjWKx0oiYHiBmgkR21F-_BGBWXdkKwsx?e=download&ax=ACxEAsZI7vKA0mvqZ8b_6ZXrjWcvALO1Ed8ISeQ8nm3gXEaDYeIvATuDvP-AFwNMeICgppChj20JCE3h1iaTS9boAfTvQolh5vQgqOpIx4wYST73RAVtvS-LzEWtXISXvWPNynSJ1U5abJk3N_74k7HSfYw5BmBWWyoQBIgZGtRA-wtAl1bOvfH-pbl1a-Jj_Gv94fyxLMcUoECyi-9SI2Y_NFadJWZx9PLOm_5JSu1_t4t4FrB6a2G2yxCOS4CPLXYbURMsu4A_sgMnkAWOUi6ySfu5m6g-R0U9ijnKxzzao4NEEjmD6YqY0iMtwtpeg5i2G9fdDye9RVrHhDPL6JatiZFR7XL_YkFAlMh7sn7xBQKS28q4wpmCwQggxAQL4yUa_oTZv3VBdeo6qQutvLiHc607RUpd8QC3PqNuVXWJ8_JjEQ2Y06iiUQqlZP-mBsLOg0VcP6quk8LAUYB5PgZKkOJeoUnJtl6PZ-40m9dDnvnoNdvui0iiyZfpZwUJqoQpi--nQu6jyJ0THQMqg_CLxYc47IdswTL_d8ibL3NBr7xBEsLSEXnVB1IbG6Dmp_TYFeCWgu3qkD7gch1gHMUd-yrAhuxySRuhwSEjY9rY7HjFwqku9NIY_HOCVzMFgLNn1ct99Ol15X34L_2GxdOvgRNZXiJQI03_bC-z6fD9B-SC7qN4J_UloURru-BZAOx5QEf50MONwuCU_MoktWmn4R3tZ31WSVSETPDGrJ9Lc1u19zQ1&authuser=0&nonce=ov43spacssu7i&user=11838597598179151713&hash=jbcln7lopb85ke1a3j31e6lo9q3ns09b). The report now includes a “consolidated profit/(loss) account that translates the GTC transactions into $USD”.

* [Why I am a Holon maximalist](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/why-i-am-holon-maximalist/10152) by [Owocki](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/owocki) introduces Holons as “Greek for “whole/parts” meaning literally the whole that is simultaneously a part, and vice versa. Holons are the circles that we live in.” “What I think is cool about a holonic point of view of Gitcoin Grants is that each of the grants in Gitcoin has a lot to gain from the multiple holons (grants rounds) it is in.”

* [Can Gitcoin be doing more to support Ethereum Protocol Devs?](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/can-gitcoin-be-doing-more-to-support-ethereum-protocol-devs/10151) by [Owocki](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/owocki) is an invitation to the Gitcoin community to participate in the recent debate on Twitter regarding funding Ethereum protocol developers. “Ethereum Protocol Development is a public good, and it’s possibly the closest one to the ecosystem that gave Gitcoin life. Can Gitcoin be doing more to support Ethereum Protocol Devs?”

* [Proof Of Impact seedling [Proposals Wanted]](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/proof-of-impact-proposals-wanted/10179) by [Owocki](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/owocki) is an extension of the [ImpactDAO Cartographers](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/impactdao-cartographers-wanted/9866) initiative - with the ambition to create a ImpactDAO RatingsDAO emerge. “Such a DAO would maintain an on-chain registry of which ImpactDAOs have what Impact”.

* [DevRel @ GitcoinDAO](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/devrel-gitcoindao/10161) by [Owocki](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/owocki) has the objective to start a conversation around what the developer relations practice at Gitcoin should look like.

### 💙 [Fraud Detection & Defense (FDD)](https://gitcoin.notion.site/Fraud-Detection-Defense-2bde13c0b8e74fda81435d94e49e2703)

* We’re working on a model within [Ethelo](https://ethelo.com) (a decentralized decision-making tool for e-democracy) to improve our grants review process. Our goal is to focus on maximizing trust in the system

* Our sybil resistance tooling is increasingly battle-tested and our community review model is working well. We will continue to improve our anti-sybil operational process (ASOP) with a combination of machine learning and human evaluation

* We are running a Contributor Experience (CX) survey and need [your feedback](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdoGB9d0SWyC-VC9Ntp4lSpFcW7v1lOrhmLHk-Yk4Zw2DRfYA/viewform)!

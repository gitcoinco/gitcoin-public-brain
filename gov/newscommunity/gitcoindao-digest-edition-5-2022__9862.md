## [🤖 Meta-Governance](https://gov.gitcoin.co/c/governance-meta/6)

* [The Steward Council - Formation & Mandate](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/the-steward-council-formation-mandate/9843) by [Simona](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/Pop) seeks to “select the members of the GD Steward Council, approve its mandate and tenure. (...) This council looks to elevate the participation of the most engaged stewards.” Currently 14 stewards are eligible for a seat on the council.

* [Treasury Diversification Part II - Welcoming Strategic Partners](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/treasury-diversification-part-ii-welcoming-strategic-partners/9825) was posted by [Dan](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/schlabach). The post is proposing that “Gitcoin diversify $15 million of its treasury into stablecoins”. If you are interested in participating please[ fill out this form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScpCS8BMyp0M6VJ9OrDRWyYr1FaQqGdsHd94AS314dEUFxvmg/viewform) or share your thoughts in the thread. An [update](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/treasury-diversification-working-group-update-2-5-2022/9830) was also shared with progress so far and the next steps forward.

* [Kyle](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/kyle) has provided a [Summary on the S13 Workstream Budget Proposals](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/summary-s13-budget-proposal-recap-kyles-voter-guide/9856) presents the TLDR of what each Workstream plans to achieve with their upcoming budget. The thread is directed towards “folks who may be struggling to digest the nuances in these proposals''.

* [How does the DAO prioritize side rounds?](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/how-does-the-dao-prioritize-side-rounds/9827) was posted by [Owocki](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/owocki) to spark a discussion on the importance of having a credible and neutral policy to ensure a smooth running of the Gitcoin Grants side rounds, with notable contributions by [Scott](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/ceresstation) and [Connor](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/connor).

  There is also an active discussion in the [Community Input on GR13 Structure](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/discussion-community-input-on-gr13-structure/9735/6)-thread regarding how our upcoming GR13 specifically will be architectured.

* [A DAO has no CEO](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/a-dao-has-no-ceo/9852) by [Owocki](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/owocki) sheds light on how GitcoinDAO has grown over the past year and goes into detail on the separation between Gitcoin Holdings (the legal entity that launched the DAO) and GitcoinDAO.

* [The Great Bear, The Great Reset, The Great Revival](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/the-great-bear-the-great-reset-the-great-revival/9855) by [Owocki](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/owocki) provides insight into Gitcoin’s history, the major challenges that we’ve overcome and how we are growing into the next phase - “The Great Revival”.

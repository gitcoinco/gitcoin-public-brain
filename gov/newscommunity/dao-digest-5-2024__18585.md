# 🔥 Hot Topics

![|720x412](upload://9zWb885VphLeQsvCdCT9pODmIM5.png)

📢 Get ready for Gitcoin Grants Round 20, which focuses on 4 OSS programs: Hackathon Alumni, dApps & Apps, Developer Tooling, and Libraries, and Web3 Infrastructure. Find out more about the [Eligibility Criteria](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gg20-oss-funding-rounds-eligibility-criteria/18393).

🪴A [GG20 Project Charter](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gg20-project-charter/18307) has been introduced, revitalizing the design and management of the Gitcoin Grants. This unfolds in [three phases](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/our-community-driven-engagement-framework-for-oss-rounds/18242):

1) Crafting Agreements in the Open;

2) Services Delivery with Community Oversight;

3) Project Closeout & Community Feedback.

🚀 The Gitcoin [Citizen Grants Program](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/deconstructing-gitcoin-citizen-grants-program-launch-details/18168) is now split into:

* Citizens Retro (Retroactive Funding). Join [this program](https://gitcoin.notion.site/Citizens-Retro-704a64ca7a874a1d97d94d48a05bb81f) if you have made contributions in pursuit of Gitcoin’s [essential intents](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/update-gitcoin-s-ratified-essential-intents-2023-2024-and-a-recap-of-our-successes/16818).
* Citizens Innovate (Citizen Proposals). Join [this program](https://gitcoin.notion.site/Citizens-Innovate-cf556fdb861143e78765410b368565c2#697c3e7550ca40ada0e7e0da16c7a94f) if you can push the boundaries of grants discovery and management with LLMs in alignment with the [Citizens Innovate RFP](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/citizens-forward-llm-rfp-pathway-2-open-innovation/17980).
* Citizens Forward (RFPs). Join [this program](https://gitcoin.notion.site/Citizens-Forward-52bb700932924eeb9c2fef9f04951a68) if you can assist with discovery of public goods and personalize the experience for users in accordance with the [Citizens Forward RFP](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/citizens-forward-llm-rfp-pathway-1-specific-deliverable/17979).

➡ ️Check out the [notion site](https://gitcoin.notion.site/Citizen-Grants-6cf45c8501c3430eae3791434bcafb87) for in-depth details and tell us what you’ve been up to on the [governance forum](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gitcoin-citizens-what-are-you-doing/16922).

🤑Don’t forget that [Gitcoin Citizens Retro Round #3](https://explorer-v1.gitcoin.co/#/round/42161/0x5aa255d5cae9b6ce0f2d9aee209cb02349b83731) is live, deployed on Allo v1 and operating on Arbitrum.

* Proposals that have on-chain steps, such as budget requests, are approved on Snapshot before moving on to Tally.

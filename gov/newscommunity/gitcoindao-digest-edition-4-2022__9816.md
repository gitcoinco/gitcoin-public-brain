## [🤖 Meta-Governance](https://gov.gitcoin.co/c/governance-meta/6)

* [Consent of the Governed (and Legitimacy by Process) in GitcoinDAO](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/consent-of-the-governed-and-legitimacy-by-process-in-gitcoindao/9778) by [Owocki](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/owocki) articulates how “Legitimacy flows in GitcoinDAO - specifically ‘legitimacy by process’. eg if a process is legitimate, the outputs of that process gain legitimacy.”

* [Schelling Point Updates](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/schelling-point-updates/9786) by [Ben](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/M0nkeyFl0wer) describes all of the latest updates and details including locations and Covid-safety rules. * The [response](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/a-vision-for-a-pluralistic-civilizational-scale-infrastructure-for-funding-public-goods/9503/11) by [Glen Weyl](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/GlenWeyl) to [Owocki](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/owocki)’s “[A Vision for A Pluralistic Civilizational Scale Infrastructure for funding Public Goods](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/a-vision-for-a-pluralistic-civilizational-scale-infrastructure-for-funding-public-goods/9503)” thread sparked a rousing discussion on quadratic funding, with interesting points made by [nollied](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/nollied/summary) and [Ben](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/m0nkeyfl0wer/summary).

They need to grow together, and not grow too far out of balance with one another.”

### 💙 [Fraud Detection & Defense (FDD)](https://gitcoin.notion.site/Fraud-Detection-Defense-2bde13c0b8e74fda81435d94e49e2703)

* Check out the [State of FDD Season 12 Review](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/state-of-fdd-season-12-review/9774), a precursor to our Season 13 budget proposal which will be posted on Friday, Feb 4 according to the [Workflow Accountability Flow](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gitcoin-dao-workstream-accountability-flow/9644/). [Danilo](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/danlessa) [clarifies](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/state-of-fdd-season-12-review/9774/7) the use of certain KPIs to measure the performance of the FDD workstream, with notable discussion points given by [Kyle](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/Kweiss), [Pavel](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/Pfed-prog), and [Joe](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/DisruptionJoe).

* We’ve conducted almost 15 hours of strategy meetings, aligning on the essential intent in each of our focus areas and the outcomes needed during the next 3 months to help us get there. You can think of these as “What would be true if FDD is successful over the next 90 days”.

* Treasury management netted us $75k in gains. The stream will end up with $30k extra after using a portion of the gains to kickstart the FDD OS, increasing the number of human evaluations, and beginning work on a red team simulation software similar to cadCAD.
  * Model and simulate collective decision outcome variations
  * Contributors can easily access data without compromising security

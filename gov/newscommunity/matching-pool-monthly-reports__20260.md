# Matching Pool Monthly Reports
With the passing of proposal [SGTM 002](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/passed-sgtm-002-avantgarde-x-gitcoin-strategic-asset-management-for-the-matching-pool/19698), the Gitcoin DAO elected Avantgarde Finance to strategically manage the assets in the Gitcoin Matching Pool (MP).

Following [SGTM 003](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/sgtm-003-approving-multisig-structure-for-avantgarde-to-manage-the-matching-pool-assets/19904), whereby the DAO approved the suggested infrastructure setup to manage the MP assets, said assets have now been **fully deployed** as per the strategy introduced in SGTM 002. 

As outlined in prop SGTM 003, the MP assets are being managed from a separate avatar safe owned by the Gitcoin Governor contract. The avatar safe and its activities remain trackable at all times at the following address:

https://etherscan.io/address/0x25A84CC1014062463a829Fbc2B0848798BF5E378

### In this thread, we'll be providing monthly updates to the community on the performance and status of the MP assets; including portfolio snapshots, allocations, and prevailing yields.

#### September Update
Gm all, sharing here an update on the Matching Pool for September.

Crypto prices moved higher in the first half of the month with ETH peaking at 4760 before moving into a period of choppy price action into month end. Funding rates followed an intuitive pattern, as strong risk appetite gave way to muted leverage demand as prices declined in the last two weeks of September. Stablecoin yields remained healthy through the majority of the period and the strategy continued to benefit from its allocation to fixed yield into the positions expiry in the last week of September. Opportunities were more muted in the last week, with the strategy maintaining more exposure to floating rates until better fixed yields present themselves.

**Total AuM for the MP as of today is $13,589,132.**

#### October Update
October proved to be a challenging test for the Matching Pool, as the broader crypto market's multi-year "Uptober" trend was decisively broken. The pool's ETH holdings were directly impacted by severe market volatility, most notably the flash crash around 10/10, which saw ETH prices tumble significantly from early-month highs of around $4700 and triggered record-breaking liquidations across the ecosystem, with ETH currently sitting around $3150. The environment has also seen impacts in DeFi as leverage demand waned, and yields on stables compressed in the second half of the month.
In an aftershock, the beginning of November saw the Balancer v2 and Stream Finance xUSD events, which led to knock on effects felt across the whole ecosystem, as DeFi liquidity was significantly impacted. [We have detailed this here in the most recent report on the DAO treasury](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/sgtm-004-generating-yield-on-dao-treasury-assets-to-support-runway/20217/27?u=avantgarde). The Matching Pool had very limited exposure at the portfolio level (~0.57% of current AUM), which has been segregated and vault tokens transferred out to original deposit address and we are pursuing recovery off-chain.

The Matching Pool now sitting at $11.5m, with ETH exposure at 63% and Stables exposure at 37%.

#### November Update
Following the volatility of October and the liquidity events early in the month, November was characterized by broad market stabilization. ETH price action has remained largely range-bound and appears to be consolidating around the $3,150 level as the market absorbed the recent deleveraging. While the acute selling pressure has subsided, risk appetite in the DeFi sector has not yet returned to Q3 levels, with funding rates remaining muted and stablecoin yields remaining compressed across major protocols.

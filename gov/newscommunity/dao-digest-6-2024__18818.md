# 🔥 Hot Topics

![|720x356](upload://wn1Zf6KcsCitG8OQkh0ts9kyuxw.png)

✅ GG20 has successfully concluded with significant improvements from GG19, excellent operational feedback and overwhelming support from the community.

🆕 GG20 introduced an external council for community grant review and governance process who [provided detailed insights and valuable feedback](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gg20-community-round-governance-a-retrospective/18766) (avg. satisfaction rating = 4.71/5) for future iterations.

📆 When initially elected, the Community Council members were elected to select the Community Rounds only for GG20. [Should this proposal pass](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/proposal-extending-the-term-of-gg-community-council/18776), we would extend that term beyond GG20 until December 31, 2024.

🤠 Gitcoin Citizens Retro #3 concluded with $55K crowdfunded, 50K GTC in matching and extra matching from @ThankArbitrum, bringing it to a total of $140K, making this the most successful round to date.

.🔄 [Read this brief overview](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gitcoin-citizens-rounds-value-generation-opportunities/18727) of how Gitcoin Citizens rounds contribute to increasing Allo GMV through strategic value loops that compound over time.

🌟 Our primary objective for 2024 is to triple our Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) from 2023, aiming for $15 million, with a Big Hairy Audacious Goal (BHAG) of reaching $50 million, @meglister outlines in [this post](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/2024-gmv-strategy-overview-grants-lab/18805).

🔦 @kyle follows the [passing of the torch tradition](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/another-passing-of-the-torch/18704), departing from official Gitcoin interactions and moving into the Passport business unit to co-lead the spin-out.

🤔 Leveraging our technology and community levers will optimize growth and catalyze meaningful change. After all, Gitcoin [has always](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/harmonizing-community-and-technology-gitcoin/18799) been about advancing the common good through public goods funding.

🌇 Public Goods Network [(PGN) is shutting down](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/pgn-shutdown-a-recap/18794). Despite calls for a new management plan, no viable long term options emerged. Still have unclaimed assets on the network? [Join the discussion](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/public-goods-network-retrospective-recommendation-discussion/18772).

💸 [Read this quick recap](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/proposal-delegate-gitcoins-glodollar-to-fund-public-goods-via-glodollar/18804) and share your thoughts on delegating $100K in GloDollars to passively fund public goods through GloDollar's "Automatic Public Goods Funding" mechanism.

🟢 The Greenpill Podcast is seeking guest co-hosts from the regen space for seasons 5 to 7! [Submit a proposal before June 3](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/greenpill-podcast-guest-seasons-call-for-proposals/18754) for a chance to co-host with @owocki and shape the future of the show.

🌎 Gitcoin contributors recently met in Lisbon for a 3-day retreat. Read the [Offsite Recap](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/lisbon-offsite-recap/18641) by @meglister for all the takeaways and stay tuned for more follow ups.

🤓 The Regenlearnings.xyz group has been steadily making progress and working in public, [checkout the latest 3 meeting recordings](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MoALFsYyrBg&list=PLvTrX8LNPbPlaAaefsyGqbGOaM9O0fT9x&t=530s) in this youtube playlist!

🎉 ICYMI the [Gitcoin 2.0 White Paper](https://gitcoin.co/whitepaper) launched while the team was at ETHDenver hosting the inaugural Ecosystem Growth Summit with Electric Capital. Top insights can be found [here](https://ecosystemgrowth.gitcoin.co/).

📹 Watch the Gitcoin 2.0 whitepaper presentation by @owocki and @meglister at the Ecosystem Growth Summit, hosted earlier this year at ETH Denver – [Grants as a Growth Vehicle](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rBFSavB82hw).

💭 After the articulation of [CASE](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/the-case-capital-allocation-strategy-exchange/18271) we are starting to see Gitcoin become a [Capital Allocation Strategy Exchange](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/case-prioritization-framework/18489).

# Ecosystem Collective

## What’s new?

Gitcoin’s community is now being fostered under the banner of the[ Ecosystem Collective](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/introducing-gitcoin-s-ecosystem-collective/17954).

![|720x369](upload://k0imbFlCg4UbZ2N1VNdzVvfRvX6.png)

* Objectives for DAO2DAO Governance StableLab Partnership:
  * Explore opportunities for DAO-to-DAO collaborations
  * Facilitate strategic partnerships and collaborative ventures with other DAOs to amplify Gitcoin’s influence and drive adoption of Gitcoin’s products within the broader Web3 ecosystem
  * Leverage Gitcoin’s governance token allocations to engage in Metagovernance efforts
  * Orchestrate token swaps with strategically aligned partners to foster mutual governance participation and ecosystem growth

* Feedback from GG20 grantees was overall positive. Some other points of info include:
  * A unique subscription was created for grantees on the Gitcoin Grants email list, with nearly 50 sign ups so far.
  * Nearly 500 people joined the twitter spaces held ahead of GG20.
  * Average email open rates (donors): 28%
  * Average email open rates (grantees): 70%
  * Over 1,000 visits to the portal since April 16

![|690x435](upload://5Qw7cR1BIXlA2Qv5BAMezKEIhLp.png)

# 🔥 Hot Topics

🎉 ICYMI the [Gitcoin 2.0 White Paper](https://gitcoin.co/whitepaper) launched while the team was at ETHDenver. We also hosted the inaugural Ecosystem Growth Summit alongside Electric Capital. The top insights from the event can be found on this brand-new microsite:[ https://ecosystemgrowth.gitcoin.co/](https://ecosystemgrowth.gitcoin.co/).

![|435x396](upload://tdsxs9TpZhTWY1fLVccBTiPEWJ.jpeg)

📹 Watch the Gitcoin 2.0 whitepaper presentation by @owocki and @meglister at ETH Denver –[ Grants as a Growth Vehicle](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rBFSavB82hw).

📹 Watch @owocki’s talk at ETH Denver – [Public Goods Funding: The New Meta](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P6TFcBEXESk).

🌐 Gitcoin’s community is now being fostered under the banner of the [Ecosystem Collective](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/introducing-gitcoin-s-ecosystem-collective/17954). The 🟣 360 Team is responsible for guiding the EC’s strategy, holding context for 🔵 Gitcoin Citizens, and acting as points of accountability for ⚪ Citizens who spin up new initiatives through the Citizen Grants program.

🚀 The Gitcoin [Citizen Grants Program](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/deconstructing-gitcoin-citizen-grants-program-launch-details/18168) is now split into Citizens Retro (Retroactive Funding), Citizens Innovate (Citizen Proposals) and Citizens Forward (RFPs). Check out the [notion site](https://gitcoin.notion.site/Citizen-Grants-6cf45c8501c3430eae3791434bcafb87) for in-depth details and tell us what you’ve been up to on the [governance forum](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gitcoin-citizens-what-are-you-doing/16922).

⏭ Do you have proposal focused on developing a tool using LLM for grant discovery? Join the [Citizen Forward Grants Program](https://gitcoin.notion.site/Citizens-Forward-52bb700932924eeb9c2fef9f04951a68) if you have a specific deliverable that aims to address any of the areas proposed in the [Citizens Forward RFP](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/citizens-forward-llm-rfp-pathway-1-specific-deliverable/17979) to help simplify the discovery of public goods and personalize the experience for users.

🆕 Do you have groundbreaking ideas that push the boundaries of what’s possible in grants discovery and management? Join the [Citizen Innovate Grants Program](https://gitcoin.notion.site/Citizens-Innovate-cf556fdb861143e78765410b368565c2#697c3e7550ca40ada0e7e0da16c7a94f) to propose unique, creative uses of LLMs that can offer fresh perspectives and solutions to existing challenges, in alignment with the [Citizens Innovate RFP](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/citizens-forward-llm-rfp-pathway-2-open-innovation/17980) to help enhance the accessibility, efficiency, and impact of discovering public goods.

🔄 Have you been retroactively supporting Gitcoin to achieve its mission and execute on its essential intents? Join the [Citizen Retro Grants Program](https://gitcoin.notion.site/Citizens-Retro-704a64ca7a874a1d97d94d48a05bb81f) if you have made contributions in the areas of educational content, governance, operations, data analysis, support, platform and protocol dev + devrel.

🤝 As Gitcoin continues to expand, we’re seeking to partner with [skilled Round Operators and Service Providers](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/expanding-our-network-of-providers/18251). Our focus is on those who can align with our mission and values, bring expertise in managing grant rounds, and offer comprehensive program management.

3️⃣ A [GG20 Project Charter](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gg20-project-charter/18307) has been introduced, as a revitalized approach to designing and managing the GG20 OSS Round, [the new framework will unfold in three phases](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/our-community-driven-engagement-framework-for-oss-rounds/18242)— 1) Crafting Agreements in the Open, 2) Services Delivery with Community Oversight, and 3) Project Closeout & Community Feedback.

🟢 It’s Capital Allocation season! Get ready for the [latest season](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I-uJlvSJe9E) of the Green Pill Podcast.

💚 Would you like to be considered to host a community round in GG20, and apply for matching from Gitcoin? Here’s the [proposal template](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gg20-community-round-proposal-template/18122) to work off of and a breakdown of the process.

🧐 The [GG20 Eligibility Criteria](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gg20-eligibility-criteria-community-rounds/17846) has been reviewed by the DAO-elected council, including a dedicated pool of funds (up to $125K quarterly) available to support “matching on matching” pools for community-led grant rounds meeting the specific criteria.

🤓 Calling all intellectually curious theorists, program designers, and data scientists to contribute to the [RegenLearnings.xyz](https://regenlearnings.xyz/) research group started by @owocki and @ccerv1.

🌟 Read ‘[A North Star for Gitcoin (and the decade-long path to get there)](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/a-north-star-for-gitcoin/17265)’ to learn more about our current course towards the north star, and how your work can support reaching it.

## What’s new?

The Grants Lab unit is focused on product development and adoption, aiming to increase total grant value through Allo platform usage and prioritizing product, developer relations, marketing, and Grants Stack operations! Check out the latest Grants Stack [Release Notes](https://gitcoin.notion.site/What-s-New-Gitcoin-Grants-Stack-8495cce451bf4bc6881539746902e7c5) 🙂

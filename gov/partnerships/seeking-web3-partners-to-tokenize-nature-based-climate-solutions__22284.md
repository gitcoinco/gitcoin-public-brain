# Seeking Web3 Partners to Tokenize Nature-Based Climate Solutions

<https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/seeking-web3-partners-to-tokenize-nature-based-climate-solutions/22284>
OasisCone.com | 2025-07-27 15:15:58 UTC | #1

Hello Gitcoin community,

We’ve developed a field-tested, patent-pending solution for afforestation in harsh desert environments — using no fresh water and enabling scalable carbon sinks via halophytes and mangroves. It’s called the **Oasis Cone**, and it's already being piloted with institutional interest in Oman, Egypt, and Morocco.

But the real momentum seems to live here — among **netizens**, not bureaucrats.

That’s why we’re actively looking to **co-create a tokenized funding model** — not just for the Oasis Cone system, but for other scalable, undervalued, nature-based solutions that struggle to attract legacy climate finance.

We’re seeking collaborators in:
- Web3 token architecture (ERC-20 or other chains)
- NFT strategy (symbolic backer tiers exist)
- Smart contract devs
- DAO launch or aligned community access
- Legal / regulatory structuring (esp. outside U.S.)

We have no interest in hype. We’re serious about real-world outcomes — desert afforestation, food security, carbon drawdown.

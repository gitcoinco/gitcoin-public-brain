[quote="Layn, post:5, topic:10273"]
* Does the DAO looks like you imagine it would be when you started it?
[/quote]

The primary thing we were trying to solve with the QuadraticLands.com retro drop experience was enabling a group of [Stewards](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/introducing-stewards-governance/41) who could be legitimate in governing Gitcoin Grants.    Most governance tokens have such low voter turnout, and by enabling delegation at the start, the idea was to evolve past this problem.  Here is who the community selected as stewards at the start of the DAO:

[![E2ey1M8UUAYFRpR|648x500, 50%](upload://AmeLK4Unf9wujB5QpMqBamGc2Tx.jpeg)](https://twitter.com/owocki/status/1398282868741214215)

 I was pretty happy with how that created a basis of legitimacy for the DAO.

Beyond the structure [that was set up at the start](https://github.com/gitcoinco/governance-docs) and [the mission](https://gitcoin.co/mission) being an organizing principle, I did not have strongly held expectations for how the DAO would work.  Like otheres, I've been trying to learn about DAOs ( see https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/dao-design-best-practices/10219 ) Egregores ( https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/the-gitcoin-gitcoindao-egregore-is-emerging/8200 ) work.   Just others, I've been trying to figure out how the dao as an entity [scales its sensemaking](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/how-do-we-scale-our-sensemaking/10192/3).

owocki | 2022-04-06 23:12:53 UTC | #9

[quote="umarkhaneth, post:8, topic:10273"]
* We have a lot of great discussions about values in DAO Vibes. As someone still learning, a lot of the values of Web3 that are discussed often seem cultural and impossible to program into our money – what are the values we can program into our money?
[/quote]

I think that with a turing complete smart contract language and oracles that put info on chain, you could plausibly program really any value into money (in theory).  But the ecosystem is only a few years old + not mature enough for that to happen yet.

In the meantime, tools for creating intersubjective consensus (like CoordinAPE, Gitcoin Grants, CLRFund) are a nice shim to make sure that financial rewards match our values.

Also checkout https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/how-do-we-scale-our-sensemaking/10192 - which I think has some novel ideas about how to measure what we value.

Hello Everyone,

My name is Manas, and I’m excited to begin contributing more actively to Gitcoin’s governance.


Professional background in government affairs & taxation (GST, India).Passion for public goods, rural development, and transparent governance systems.

Now exploring how DAOs like Gitcoin can become models for accountability, inclusivity, and impact-driven funding.

My Governance Focus

I’d like to support Gitcoin DAO by contributing to:

1. Transparency – Clear reporting on proposals, funding allocations, and outcomes.

2. Accountability – Defining KPIs, safeguards, and fair review processes.

3. Inclusivity – Ensuring both GTC holders and Citizens have strong voices in governance.

Why Gitcoin

Gitcoin is a pioneer in public goods funding, and I believe my experience in policy & compliance can help strengthen community trust in how resources are allocated and reported.

How I’ll Contribute

Comment on proposals with a focus on KPIs, risk checks, and reporting mechanisms.

Support discussions that bridge GTC holders, Stewards, and Citizens.

Explore small Citizen Grant projects (like transparency dashboards) to deliver visible impact.

Looking forward to learning, engaging, and building alongside this community.
Happy to collaborate with anyone working on governance frameworks, accountability tools, or transparency reporting.

# Consider Migrating Gitcoin DAO Governance to Degov

<https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/consider-migrating-gitcoin-dao-governance-to-degov/25202>
bear-wang | 2026-03-25 06:46:57 UTC | #1

Hello Gitcoin community,

I'm Bear Wang, from the DeGov team. I have been following Gitcoin for a while and I am impressed by the community and the work you have done. As Tally will be shutting down its services soon https://x.com/tallyxyz/status/2033917127304814802, Gitcoin needs to find an alternative platform for governance. I would like to suggest considering Degov as a potential replacement.


DeGov https://github.com/ringecosystem/degov is an open-source UI interface for the OpenZeppelin Governor contract, which is widely used for on-chain governance. I noticed that Gitcoin DAO is also currently using the OpenZeppelin Governor contract. This means that migrating to DeGov would be relatively straightforward, as it is designed to solve this exact use case. You can check the existing DAOs on DeGov Square https://square.degov.ai/ to see how it works.

As for price and deployment, DeGov is open-source and free to use and self-host by following https://docs.degov.ai/integration/overview/. Alternatively, you can choose our hosting service at a reasonable price (see https://degov.ai/pricing). Recently, some DAOs have already decided to consider migrating to DeGov, such as Lisk https://forum.lisk.com/t/test-proposal-2-a-new-voting-platform/678, so I think it is worth considering for Gitcoin DAO as well.

If you have any questions about DeGov or want to discuss the migration process, please feel free to reach out. I am happy to provide more information and assist in any way I can.

owocki | 2022-05-21 16:03:05 UTC | #3

The GitcoinDAO Governance system is a fork of COMP/UNI style governance (not a multisig).  Tokens are released from the [GTC timelock](https://etherscan.io/address/0x57a8865cfb1ecef7253c27da6b4bc3daee5be518) via [the governance process](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gitcoin-dao-governance-process-v3/10358) .   I'd recommending skipping to [this part](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gitcoin-dao-governance-process-v3/10358#gitcoins-gov-alpha-details-22) of the docs which contains the answer to your question.

Also worth checking out the [governance-docs](https://github.com/gitcoinco/governance-docs)  and [launch blog post](https://gitcoin.co/blog/introducing-gtc-gitcoins-governance-token/).

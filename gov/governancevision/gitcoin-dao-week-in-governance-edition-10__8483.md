# Gitcoin DAO - Week in Governance, Edition #10

<https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gitcoin-dao-week-in-governance-edition-10/8483>
ceresstation | 2021-09-03 23:07:04 UTC | #1

This weekly post has the ambition to summarize what’s been going on in the Gitcoin DAO Governance. Topics, ideas, proposals, and votes will be listed with links for further reading and discussion. If there’s anything missing please reach out! 📝

### [🤖 Meta-Governance](https://gov.gitcoin.co/c/governance-meta/6)

* A [vote](https://snapshot.org/#/gitcoindao.eth/proposal/QmZYrZKBaExvZsnNNfKbFixJe2C7PNY96DtoF1tQtdNhLN) to issue a 40k GTC grant to CLR.fund is up on Snapshot after two weeks of [discussion](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/proposal-issue-clr-fund-a-40k-gtc-grant/8240).
* A [vote](https://snapshot.org/#/gitcoindao.eth/proposal/QmSRTDaRckWkWBBAHDXXyatMFLUZcFjSCkvHSBuC84fgbf) by 🔥_🔥 is live on Snapshot to ratify the GR11 Matching Pool Allocations proposed and discussed in this [forum thread](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gitcoin-round-11-matching-pool-allocations/8386).
* The discussion following the [proposal](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/proposal-to-adjust-code-of-conduct-wrt-airdrops/8383/) authored by @owocki to Adjust the [Gitcoin Code of Conduct](https://support.gitcoin.co/kb/article/42-code-of-conduct/) WRT Airdrops is ongoing. @tra38 authored a new [thread](http://tra38) to discuss how a ban on airdrops could be enforced with active discussion.
* @owocki authored a thread on [GitcoinDAO META: Coordination (of Culture + Capital](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gitcoindao-meta-coordination-of-culture-capital/8437)).
* @kyle provided some [details](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gitcoin-round-11-matching-pool-allocations/8386/6) in the discussion on [Gitcoin Round 11 Matching Pool Allocations](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gitcoin-round-11-matching-pool-allocations/8386/5) with stats from previous grants round with SE Asia subcategories. @tjayrush added a [summary of](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gitcoin-round-11-matching-pool-allocations/8386/8) the proposed changes.
* @owocki authored a [thread](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/september-2021-whos-hiring/8468) to highlight open positions in the Workstreams. @ceresstation [posted](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/september-2021-whos-hiring/8468/2) a link to the new onboarding [Typeform](https://form.typeform.com/to/E96KbTSy) for GitcoinDAO contributors.

### [🧙‍♂️🧙‍♀️ Workstream Discussion](https://gov.gitcoin.co/c/workstream-discussion/9)

[Public Goods Funding / Treasury Management](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/workstream-public-goods-funding/129)

* The transaction from the Gitcoin DAO treasury towards funding for the Public Goods Workstream has been [executed](https://www.withtally.com/governance/gitcoin/proposal/2) on-chain!
* The treasury management doc by @HelloShreyas is still [up for discussion](https://twitter.com/llamacommunity_/status/1427898604493041673), please leave comments!
* Judging for the [public goods RFP](https://gitcoin.co/blog/seeking-a-new-kind-of-public-good/) continues with some excellent submissions so far
* Discussions are starting to take shape around how DAOs at large should define the kinds of public goods they care about, in the context of [Uniswap’s side round ](https://twitter.com/uniswapgrants/status/1433212170876108800)and other current events
* Some new Discord bots / community management tools are starting to take shape

[Public Goods Prototyping (Moonshot Collective)](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/workstream-public-goods-prototyping/130)

* @[ThomasCrown](https://gov.gitcoin.co/u/ThomasCrown) authored a thread called [Moonshot Bot Gifting Program](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/moonshot-bot-gifting-program/8481) with the question; “How do we incentivize additional mints and strong sales for MSB, to maximize their usefulness as public goods funding tools?”.
* Our biggest visible accomplishment this week is the ramp-up to launch tip.party
* We’re also upgrading the tech, content, and team behind scaffold-eth
* We’re putting together a media strategy, more on this soon!
* We’re building a foundation of scalable pipelines and playbooks, the results will be invisible, but have many knock-on effects that help keep our project velocity high

[Decentralization Workstream](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/workstream-decentralize-gitcoin/180)

* The transaction from the Gitcoin DAO treasury towards funding for the Decentralization Workstream has been [executed](https://www.withtally.com/governance/gitcoin/proposal/3) on-chain.
* The first MVP of decentralized grants is close to completion to be showcased for a “Gitcoin Building Gitcoin” side round!

💙 [Fraud Detection & Defense (FDD-WG)
](https://www.notion.so/gitcoin/Fraud-Detection-Defense-f1a2969a9f324f57a02ef69a60b25cae)

* Updated all FDD events on add-event calendar with new zoom links (please delete and resubscribe to current events if you already had it)!
* New contributors are starting to be onboarded in preparation for Grants Round 11 defense
* Blockscience continues to put out [great research](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3907693), this time on the role of algorithms in governance

[Merch, Memes, Marketing Workstream](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/workstream-suggestion-mmm-merch-memes-marketing-for-public-goods-gitcoin-gitcoindao/7066)

* The first [Memepalooza](https://www.addevent.com/event/ys8333859) event took place in Discord this week. The event was a collaboration between Moonshot Collective and MMM.
* The MMM Workstream onboarding experience for new contributors has seen an overhaul with a new [Typeform](https://form.typeform.com/to/YuA2wHkg). A second Typeform to [request](https://form.typeform.com/to/iM5lAFFD) memes, merch, marketing or anything between, is also live.

That’s it for this week in Gitcoin DAO governance. Feel free to join the discussions via the links above and on [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/gitcoin), and visit the [Gitcoin DAO Event calendar](https://www.addevent.com/calendars/pz351122/) to keep yourself updated on what’s to come!

# Partnership Proposal: Orange Studio x Gitcoin DAO — Creative Execution for Builders & Grantees

Funnelalchemist | 2025-07-02 09:24:11 UTC | #1

Hello Gitcoin DAO team,

My name is Solomon Terseer David, Sales Lead at Orange Studio, a leading Web3-native creative studio with a strong track record in branding, animation, UI/UX, and front-end development for blockchain projects.

I'm reaching out to propose a strategic partnership with Gitcoin DAO to support your builders, grantees, and ecosystem projects with high-quality creative services that help them move from idea to market-ready product.

What We Offer:
We specialize in transforming technically sound MVPs into polished, user-friendly, and fundable products through:

**Brand Identity & Storytelling** (Logo, pitch decks, mascots, messaging systems)

**UI/UX Design** (DApp interfaces, dashboards, mobile dApps, DAO tools)

**Animation & Motion Design** (Explainer videos, token launches, motion UX)

**Front-End Development** (React-based dApps, NFT UIs, wallet integrations)

I've attached a full proposal outlining how we can partner with Gitcoin DAO to support grantees, hackathon winners, incubated teams, or even community tool builders who need top-tier creative execution to reach the next level.

We believe this partnership would greatly increase the success rate and visibility of the Gitcoin ecosystem's builders especially those who may lack access to high-quality design resources.

We’d love to explore how this could work and are open to grants, bounty structures, or direct contributor collaboration models.

Thanks for your time and the incredible work Gitcoin DAO continues to do for the public goods space.

Attachment: **[Orange Studio x Gitcoin DAO – Creative Partnership Proposal](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1DNFg0EsGm-4Zy9IvM2BJQigEhayQ0rbJ6RLE4bZ1VPo/edit?usp=sharing)**

PS: You can find our POW and all the amazing things we've done for the past decade in the attachment just above.

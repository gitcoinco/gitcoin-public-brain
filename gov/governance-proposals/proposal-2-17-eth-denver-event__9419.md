## Motivation

ETHDenver and Gitcoin have historically been very intertwined, with Gitcoin being a “Founding Steward” of ETHDenver during its first year (2018). ETHDenver and Gitcoin are both promoters of the blockchain space in Colorado, and have a positive-sum relationship that has lasted several years. This proposal ratifies that relationship, and karmically accounts for the fact that ETHDenver was not part of the initial retroactive distribution.

Some history of the relationship with ETHDenver here:

1. Gitcoin is a founding member of ETHDenver in 2018.
2. During ETHDenver 2019, Gitcoin provided Kudos NFTs to the winner of the hackathon and also Gitcoin Bufficorn avatars to attendees.
3. During ETHDenver 2019, Gitcoin was granted a comp’d sponsorship of the Event.
4. During ETHDenver 2020, Gitcoin was granted a comp’d sponsorship of the Event, and a discounted rate to do Sustain Web3 ( https://web3.sustainoss.org/ ) at ETHDenver.
5. During ETHDenver 2021, Gitcoin was granted a comp’d sponsorship of the Event.
6. During ColoradoJAM 2021, Gitcoin was given access to the Colorado Governors office to pitch Quadratic Funding to the States Department of Technology.

ETHDenver and Gitcoin have historically had a very “positive sum game” relationship, with a “pay it forward” mantra, and this grant is in that spirit. The relationship is not typically viewed as transactional by any party, but if one were to put on that lense when evaluating this proposal, I’m sure this proposal will not hurt Gitcoin’s continued access to comp’d sponsorships.

This proposal is an opportunity for GitcoinDAO to give back to the Ethereum ecosystem, get its message out there, and to network with leaders of other DAOs.

## Specification

This is a proposal to cover GitcoinDAO’s engagement at ETHDenver 2022.

1. specifically, to give Hunt Frye Web3 Services LLC (address 0x3B734d9f4759F24afa771c1Fef4Df1AEa6498360) 250 GTC to administer the ETHDenver engagements in February 2022.
2. to give 4113 GTC to the ETHDenver/SporkDAO mainnet address: 0x11CCE80E6EA89C7DA94F670977edBF7673FdCeF4.

When this proposal is setup on tally, we could perhaps set it up as a multisend so that the funds go directly to those parties directly.

## Benefits

This proposal takes the relationship with ETHDenver to the next level. The relationship with ETHDenver (now sporkDAO ) is very “pay it forward”-centric, not transactional - But if you view this in a transactional lense, I’m sure that this proposal passing will not hurt Gitcoins ability to receive comp’d sponsorships in the future. It is possible (but not guaranteed) that SporkDAO will return the favor by giving SPORK to GitcoinDAO.

This proposal is an opportunity for GitcoinDAO to give back to the Ethereum ecosystem, get its message out there, put GitcoinDAO logo side by side with talks by amazing luminaries from the space (Vitalik, Juan Benet, Austin Griffith) and to network with leaders of other DAOs.

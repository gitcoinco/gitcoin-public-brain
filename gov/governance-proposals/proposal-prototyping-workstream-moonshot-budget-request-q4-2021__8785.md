# [Proposal] Prototyping Workstream (🚀 Moonshot) Budget Request - Q4 2021

<https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/proposal-prototyping-workstream-moonshot-budget-request-q4-2021/8785>
austingriffith | 2021-10-11 19:04:18 UTC | #1

**Hey everyone,**

This is a request to fund the [Moonshot Collective](https://moonshotcollective.space/) with GTC from GitcoinDAO Treasury for the next 3 months.

The moonshot collective is a collection of builders & web3 community members who are looking to prototype experiments in coordination (whether thats public goods, private goods, governance tools). By funding the moonshot collective, we’re creating a cryptoeconomic force for builders in the space to create tools for GitcoinDAO and the ecosystem at large.

[During Q3](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/prototyping-workstream-moonshot-budget-request/8074), the collective received 40k GTC, which accomplished the following

1. The launch of [Moonshot Bots](http://bots.moonshotcollective.space/) which generated $2.2mm for the Gitcoin Grants multisig.
2. The creation of [Quadratic Diplomacy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HPsV5fQxI2k&list=PLvTrX8LNPbPmSRCXpYkxh_gO21ZfPFtCP&index=4&t=6s), a decentralized tool for distributed rewards to workstreams.
3. The creation of [Tip.Party](https://tip.party/), a tool for rewarding engagement in the DAO.
4. The creation of a [half dozen other tools](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLvTrX8LNPbPmSRCXpYkxh_gO21ZfPFtCP) other tools for DAO-era Coordination.

For Q4, we plan:

1. Another NFT-for-public-goods launch.
2. To guide Quadratic Diplomacy towards a partnership with CoordinAPE, which creates more distribution for our tools.
3. To wrap up Tip.Party, so we can allocate resources to new projects.
4. To increase the number of developers in the collective by 30%.
5. Build even more coordination tools as we need them!

We propose that 100k of GTC be deployed to **0x230Fc981F7CaE90cFC4ed4c18F7C178B239e5F9F** - a multisig of the Moonshot Collective Workstream Owners.

Along with the [Moonshot Collective Gitcoin Grant](https://gitcoin.co/grants/3004/moonshot-collective), this budget will allow us to meet our objective of supporting rapid prototyping in the space and in GitcoinDAO.

Signed,

@AustinGriffith 🎀
@Owocki 🤖

and the rest of the 🚀 Moonshot Collective.

Hi all, Here are the season 1 build roadmap for moonshot collective:

0. Another NFT Drop, which should be going live mid to late October 2021, and hopefully will raise $$$ for public goods ([just like Moonshot Bots did](https://decrypt.co/79606/moonshot-bots-nfts-gitcoin-ethereum-grants-1-8-million)).
1. [dRecruiter](https://trello.com/c/KuhoixA7/22-drecruiter-decentralized-linkedin-recruiter-protocol-where-you-can-unlock-developer-resumes-pay-the-devs-for-unlock-keys) - a Decentralized LinkedIn Recruiter - Developers can upload their resume and sell access to their contact info.  Recruiters can search candidates by keyword + purchase contact info for ETH; 80% goes to dev, 20% to protocol.
2. [CoordinAPE Partnership](https://trello.com/c/i5ihnJbA/128-combine-qd-and-coordinape-v1) - We have arranged a partnership with the CoordinAPE team to build out their roadmap of coordination tools in exchange for tokens.
3. [Gitcoin Grants GTC-based Curation Tool](https://trello.com/c/72XYdqNY/60-create-a-gitcoin-grants-curation-tool) - a way of curating Gitcoin Grants based upon cryptoeconomics.  An example use case would be to stake your GTC on which grants you think are quality, and those grants will get increased visibility in the Grants interface.   This solves the problem of the "horserace" at the start of the round where projects simply compete for more matching funds in a runaway sprint.
4. [Decentralized Sybil Resistence Aggregator](https://trello.com/c/2kKz4Ygt/130-a-decentralized-sybil-resistence-aggregator-https-proofofpersonhoodcom) - basically a decentralized version of proofofpersonhood.com - allows dApps to get the sybil score (price of forgery) for any visitor in 1-2 lines of code.  We think this tool can help usher in more sybil resistance in web3.

# Treasury Diversification Working Group Update - 3/19/2022

<https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/treasury-diversification-working-group-update-3-19-2022/10128>
schlabach | 2022-03-19 13:18:11 UTC | #1

**Treasury Diversification Working Group Update - 3/19/2022**

**Update from Last Week**

* Reached out to more DAOs, VCs, and other potential candidates to have initial conversations
* Narrowed down potential lead partners and scheduled calls with them.
* Continued our weekly working group call to sync on updates and discuss terms and partnership structure.
* Explored various pricing options for the diversification.
* Continued initial calls with interested strategic partners.

**Plan for This Week**

* Process any new form submissions.
* Continue to have initial calls and follow up calls with partners.
* Discuss any updates in our weekly meeting.
* Share any conflicts of interest (as defined in our Ethics Policy in [forum post 2](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/treasury-diversification-part-ii-welcoming-strategic-partners/9825)).

**Asks from the Community**
If you’d like to recommend a candidate strategic partner, please leave a comment on the second forum post or contact a member of the working group. If you have other questions or feedback, please feel free to leave a comment on this thread!

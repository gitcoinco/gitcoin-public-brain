# Clarifying BLEEP's Position: Aligned with Ethereum, Exploring Beyond

<https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/clarifying-bleeps-position-aligned-with-ethereum-exploring-beyond/22301>
M.attahir | 2025-07-28 01:58:11 UTC | #1

Hey Gitcoin community,

I’m Muhammad, founder of BLEEP — a self-amending, AI-native, quantum-secure blockchain project.

Recently, a comment suggested that BLEEP’s stated vision to “surpass Ethereum” implies opposition or disalignment. I want to take a moment to clarify what we actually mean and how BLEEP is deeply inspired by and intends to build upon Ethereum's legacy.

Here's how BLEEP leverages Ethereum:

Interoperability: Cross-chain bridging with Ethereum assets and contracts.

Shared Wallet Ecosystem: MetaMask, WalletConnect, and others will be native onboarding tools.

EVM Compatibility: A future compatibility layer is planned so Solidity dApps can run seamlessly on BLEEP.

Governance Synergy: Using ZK-based mechanisms to enable shared or cross-ecosystem governance.

Public Goods Ethos: Like Ethereum, BLEEP supports open-source devs and is exploring Gitcoin-style quadratic funding for builders.

Ethereum as a Foundation, Not a Competitor: We see Ethereum as the root layer that inspired many of our innovations. We aim to extend the frontier, not replace it.


Our “surpassing” vision is about exploring new design space, particularly in AI-native and quantum-resistant systems — not about displacing Ethereum, but complementing and evolving it.

I'm open to feedback and would love to engage with any members of the Gitcoin or ETH-aligned community who see room for collaboration.

Thank you 🙏
Muhammad (Founder, BLEEP)

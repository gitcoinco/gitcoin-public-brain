# [Amended Proposal]: GG24 Domains and Gitcoin Matching Pre-Ratification (Revision)

### Summary

This amended proposal requests community approval for updated Domain allocations and Gitcoin matching fund commitments for GG24. While a vote on this matter was [previously completed](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gg24-domains-and-gitcoin-matching-pre-ratification/23794), subsequent changes in funding commitments and adjustments in domain structuring have made it necessary to re-submit for ratification via a new Snapshot vote.

The purpose of this re-vote is to ensure full transparency, maintain governance legitimacy, and align the approved allocations with current realities on funding, operations, and co-funding partnerships.

**Changes from Original Vote:** The original proposal approved $1,255,000 in Gitcoin matching. This amendment reduces the total to $1,175,000 (-$60,000) through: removing Web3 Popups domain (-$75,000), adding Privacy domain (+$100,000), reducing Interop (-$100,000), and increasing Targeted Development (+$15k)."

### Rationale for Amendment

1. **Changed Funding Landscape** – Several external funding commitments got adjusted post-vote, necessitating updated Gitcoin match levels.
2. **Operational Adjustments** – Certain domain operator responsibilities and partnerships shifted after the original proposal.
3. **Community Governance Integrity** – As per governance agreements for gitcoin.eth, we are re-submitting for community approval.
4. **The push for Privacy** - It was concluded that due to incomplete commitments, [Outcome Based Funding for Web3 Popups](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/outcome-based-funding-for-web3-popups-gg24-sensemaking-report/23054/21) will move from domain into participating in a domain as a project. The operations & funding for a Privacy domain became possible so the team decided it was worth it to push for this domain to go live in GG24.

### Proposal for Community Vote

We request the community to vote on the following:

1. Yes – Approve the amended matching fund allocations for GG24, totaling $1,175,000.
2. No – Do not approve the amended allocations.
3. Abstain

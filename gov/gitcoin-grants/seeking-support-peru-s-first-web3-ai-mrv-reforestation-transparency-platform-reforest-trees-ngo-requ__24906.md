Hello Gitcoin community,

We are **REFOREST TREES**, a nonprofit organization working in the central Amazonian region of Peru (Chanchamayo – Satipo) and in high-Andean communities affected by deforestation and land degradation.
For years, we have operated with minimal resources, relying on volunteer work, Indigenous alliances, and community-driven ecological restoration.

Today, we are building something unprecedented in the region:

**Peru’s First Web3 + AI + MRV Reforestation Transparency Platform**

Our goal is to create one of Latin America’s most transparent, scientifically robust, and community-driven forest restoration systems — powered by **Web3**, **AI**, **satellite-based MRV**, and **participatory ecological monitoring**.

This platform is fully described in our 58-section technical specification document:

[PROYECTO WEB 3.0](https://reforestrees.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/PROYECTO-WEB-3.0-–-REFOREST-TREES-INGLES.11.pdf) – REFOREST TRE…

**What We Are Creating**

**Web3 Infrastructure**

* Smart contracts for donation traceability
* NFT certificates linked to each tree’s unique ID
* Environmental-impact tokenization (CO₂, biomass, area restored)
* Transparent on-chain ledger with immutable evidence and timestamps

**MRV (Measurement, Reporting, Verification)**

* NDVI/EVI satellite vegetation analysis
* Fire, drought, flood, and pest-risk detection
* AI-driven growth prediction and anomaly detection
* Timestamped before/after photo evidence
* Monthly MRV cycles aligned with ISO 14064 and Verra/Gold-Standard methodologies

**Scientific CO₂ Modeling**

* Species-specific biomass equations
* Soil and climate parameters
* Multi-year sequestration projections
* Compatibility with future carbon-credit markets (Verra, Gold Standard, ART-TREES, OPF)

**Field & Community Applications**

* Mobile app for field technicians
* GPS-verified evidence capture
* Offline data collection
* Technician digital signatures
* Community validation module (QR, wallet, PIN)

**Dashboards**

* Donor dashboard (photos, GPS, CO₂ impact)
* Corporate ESG dashboard (reports, widgets, certificates)
* Auditor panel
* WordPress public layer for full transparency

**Scientific Community Empowerment Model**
![Maps-reforest-trees|689x494](upload://89D9J9ha9oHzRZ7AYNbMmrLdivE.jpeg)

*(Shipibo-Konibo, Asháninka & Highland Communities)*

A defining element of our approach is **community-centered ecological governance**, grounded in science, Indigenous knowledge, and participatory decision-making.

**1. Local Ecological Monitoring as a Scientific Workforce**

Members of **Shipibo-Konibo**, **Asháninka**, and Andean rural communities are trained and hired as:

* Field technicians
* MRV monitors
* Evidence recorders
* Tree-growth observers
* Satellite-data validation assistants

This transforms restoration from an external intervention into a **locally governed ecological process**.

**2. Sustainable Green-Economy Employment**

The project creates long-term work through:

* Native-plant nurseries
* Seed collection & ecological propagation
* Reforestation brigades
* Soil regeneration activities
* Monthly monitoring & data validation
* Maintenance and replacement cycles

These roles provide **stable green jobs** in regions with limited economic opportunities.

**3. Digital Empowerment & Web3 Inclusion**

Communities gain access to:

* Mobile MRV tools
* Geo-tagged evidence systems
* Transparent dashboards showing their ecological contributions
* On-chain verifiable impact

This reduces the digital divide and integrates Indigenous knowledge into the global climate-tech ecosystem.

**4. Participatory Governance & Social MRV**

Our governance matrix includes:

* Indigenous leaders
* Community validators
* Field technicians
* NGO supervisors
* External auditors

This ensures distributed decision-making and reinforces environmental-justice principles.

**5. Scientific Rationale**

Peer-reviewed research confirms that forests managed by Indigenous and local communities exhibit:

* Higher ecological resilience
* Lower deforestation rates
* More accurate long-term monitoring
* Higher survival rates in reforestation efforts

Thus, community participation is not only ethical — it is **scientifically optimal**.

🧭 What is Drift Escapes?

A floating infrastructure ecosystem that blends:

* 🌐 Immersive tourism pods (luxury floating stays, NFT-backed rooms, modular design)
* 🧠 Drift Academy (youth education & content creation programs)
* 🍽️ Floating food halls with rotating cultural chefs
* 🎥 Holographic storytelling domes + media production stages
* 🌱 Sustainable marine tech + farming integration
* 💸 DriftCoin (crypto) to power creator wallets, landless ownership, and transparent revenue sharing

This is not just a resort — it’s a scalable, decentralized nation model built for creators, tech partners, and purpose-driven brands.

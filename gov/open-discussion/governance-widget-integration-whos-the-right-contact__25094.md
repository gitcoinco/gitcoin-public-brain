# Governance Widget Integration – Who's the Right Contact?

<https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/governance-widget-integration-whos-the-right-contact/25094>
DavidLen | 2026-01-31 02:04:45 UTC | #1

Hi Gitcoin community,

I’m one of the devs behind the Aave Governance widget, which displays live vote status directly inside forum discussions (quorum progress, countdown timers, and results). This lets delegates immediately see when a proposal is live for voting without switching between the forum and governance platforms, improving participation and reducing friction.

We believe Gitcoin could similarly benefit from an integration, especially given your community-driven governance model and active delegate participation. I’d love to chat about a potential integration.

**Who is the right person or working group to speak with about this?** Happy to share more details and a demo.

Tagging @owocki @MathildaDV @shawn16400 @umarkhaneth  for governance and technical insight.

Thanks

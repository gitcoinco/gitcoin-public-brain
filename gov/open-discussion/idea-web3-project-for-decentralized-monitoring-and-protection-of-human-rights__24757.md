# Idea: Web3 Project for Decentralized Monitoring and Protection of Human Rights

<https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/idea-web3-project-for-decentralized-monitoring-and-protection-of-human-rights/24757>
Dskntik | 2025-10-28 02:47:32 UTC | #1

Proposal for Gitcoin Open Discussion

Project Overview  
We propose building a decentralized Web3 platform on the Polygon blockchain, leveraging smart contracts, tokens, and DAO mechanisms for:  
- global monitoring of human rights violations;  
- coordination of mutual aid;  
- transparent funding and voting on initiatives;  
- incentivizing activists through tokenized participation.  

The platform will be open, autonomous, and global, governed by the principle of “one person — one vote”.

---

The Problem  
Thousands of human rights violations are recorded daily worldwide — driven by hatred, ignorance, or political/economic interests. Existing mechanisms (UN, NGOs, courts) are often:  
- slow;  
- government-dependent;  
- resource-constrained;  
- unable to address local or anonymous cases.  

There is no global, decentralized, trustless platform that enables:  
- rapid response;  
- tamper-proof evidence collection;  
- coordinated assistance;  
- community-driven decision-making.

---

The Solution  
“Human Rights Policy DAO” — a decentralized space for activists, victims, and allies.  

Core Features:  
1. Communication — secure chats, forums, region- or topic-based channels.  
2. Monitoring — upload of photos, videos, and documents.  
3. Anonymous Complaints.  
4. Mutual Aid:  
   - legal, psychological, and financial support;  
   - locating safe shelter, food, and water;  
   - physical protection (via nearby participants).  
5. Initiative Funding — transparent grants and community voting.  
6. Risk Alerts — interactive risk map and verified “violator watchlist”.  

---

Tokenomics (on Polygon)  
- Token: $RIGHTS (ERC-20).  
- Daily distribution to verified participants — equivalent to a basic living stipend in tokens.  
- Voting: 1 person = 1 vote (sybil-resistant verification without revealing personal data).  
- Minimum token threshold for participation in governance.  
- Automatic deductions:  
  - 5% → Stabilization Fund (reserve).  
  - 5% → Operational Expenses (development, moderation, audits).  
- Anti-spam: posting complaints or content requires a small token fee.  
- Voluntary token burning — to support deflation.  

---

Development Roadmap  
1. MVP Interface (web + mobile).  
2. Token Prototype + Mechanics Testing (distribution, voting, transfers).  
3. Smart Contract Audit.  
4. Deployment on Polygon Mainnet.  
5. Beta Testing (closed community).  
6. Full Public Launch.  

---

Key Technical Challenge  
Verifying human uniqueness without collecting personal data.  
Possible solutions:  
- PoH (Proof of Humanity) or similar protocols.  

---

Advantages  
- Full decentralization of decision-making.  
- Censorship- and shutdown-resistant.  
- Transparent funding flows.  
- Globally accessible.  
- Token incentives for sustained participation.

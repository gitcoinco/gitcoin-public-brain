I totally agree after experiencing a Blockchain game on the optimism networks last year called OP craft.
We were able to test the net work, which is now the main net work on optimism…
It was an amazing experience, and should set the standard for the industry, especially if a lot of companies and developers are using the OP stack technology to build their Blockchain on top of and develop their dapps..

Having a way to track a participants information on chain in a visual map representation, and looking at the behavior of the users in the game is always the most interesting part after the fact…

We plan on expanding our own version of this virtual world, which is essentially a Minecraft game on a Blockchain server…

Also, having the ability to create custom and complex smart contracts to alter gameplay and enhance the user experience has come to fruition with this game that I was a part of on optimism last October in 2023.

We had two weeks to build on the test that and then in the middle of the experience, they announced 30,000 OP tokens were being given away for certain categories..

During this time, someone took over the land in the center of the spawning zone, and we had to sign a treaty with the alliance through a smart contract to gain possession of our land back, but the good thing is we also gained unlimited resources by doing so in agreement to build a amazing utopia together.!

People would take your land and you could take it back if you worked hard enough, so it was a very free environment and was the first time that I felt like I was in a truly autonomous and decentralized world with no rules other than smart contracts…

We won 1000 op tokens for the educational category.

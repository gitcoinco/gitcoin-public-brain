# Gitcoin Governance Forum: Year-by-Year Arc (2021 → 2026)

<https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gitcoin-governance-forum-year-by-year-arc-2021-2026/25252>
owocki | 2026-05-10 05:12:20 UTC | #1

Been experimenting with an AI second brain, and I’ve ingested all the gitcoin gov posts into it.  Heres what it says the arc of Gitcoin DAO is year by year.

The following written by Claude Opus 4.7

Sourced from raw/gov/ (1,625 topics across 11 categories). Volume by year: 2021: 343 · 2022: 375  2023: 337 · 2024: 315 · 2025: 227 · 2026: 28 (partial).

2021 — DAO Genesis & The Workstream Era

The forum is born alongside the DAO. The single highest-viewed thread ever (76K views) is
“Introducing Stewards Governance.” Energy is centrifugal — GTC has just launched and the question is how do we govern this thing.

* Workstreams sprout everywhere: Moonshot Collective (dApp prototyping), Fraud Detection & Defense, Public Goods Funding & Treasury Mgmt, “Decentralize Gitcoin.”
* Foundational docs land: GTC Governance v0, DAO Governance Process v2, Forum Guidelines, “A Visionfor Pluralistic Civilizational Scale Infrastructure for Public Goods.”
* First drama: AKITA donation — what does the multisig do with millions in meme tokens?
* GR12 matching pool allocations — last big “GR” round in classic form.

2022 — Workstreams Mature, Protocol Work Begins

Governance scales up; the operational DAO is in full swing.

* Gitcoin Foundation (Cayman) proposed to give the DAO IRL standing.
* Governance Process v3 — workstreams formalized, S14 budgets, the “Aqueduct” funding flow.
* Grants 2.0 discussion — first signal that a protocol (not a website) is the future.
* Sybil & SBT discourse — Vitalik-adjacent Soulbound Tokens, characterizing the Sybil resistance problem.
* Sustainability anxiety: “DAO Compensation Sustainability ⏳” — first warning shots about burn vs. treasury.
* Greenpill and ImpactDAO book, Impact Certificates — retro funding seeded conceptually.
* The Future of Moonshot Collective — first workstream sunsets.

2023 — Protocol Era / Decentralization

Gitcoin transitions from a centralized QF website to Grants Stack + Allo Protocol.

* Grants Stack & Allo product overviews ship.
* Beta Round → GG18 → GG19 — independent rounds, community multisig payouts ratified one by one.
* “Decentralizing the Gitcoin Grants Program” — the explicit thesis post.
* GCPs (Gitcoin Citizen Proposals) standardize: Citizens Round, Zuzalu sponsorship, Infinite Garden TCG.
* Glo Dollar swap ($100K matching → Glo) — treasury starts experimenting.
* “GTC — After 2 Years” reflective post; new forum welcome message rolled out.

2024 — “End Game” Reset (Kyle)

The pivotal narrative reset. Kyle posts “Gitcoin Entering the End Game” (24K views, top thread of theyear).

* Gitcoin = Grants. Grants Labs business unit formed. North star: $1B distributed through Allo.
* PGN spun down (EoL June 2024).
* Passport prepared for spin-out.
* SGTM 001: $5M GTC → stables — treasury de-risks.
* GG20 runs (OSS + Community rounds, Sybil strategy, matching results).
* Citizen Grants program formalized as ongoing.
* “Addressing immediate gaps in DAO governance” — admission that the org structure has eroded.
* Tone: contraction, focus, survival.

2025 — Strategy Sprint & The Owocki Return

The biggest single thread in forum history lands: “\[GITCOIN 3.0\] Strategy Sprint \[OWOCKI EDITION\]” - 38K views. The forum becomes a public arena for re-founding.

* Owocki forks Reena’s sprint, calling Gitcoin 2.3 “incremental” and arguing for a deeper pivot. GG24 deliberately delayed three months for the reset.
* Grants Stack sunset (EoL May 2025) — the protocol bet is unwound.
* GG23 runs as a predictive/retro funding experiment — first big mechanism shift away from pure QF.
* Treasury survival mode: “It’s time for Gitcoin to get profitable,” SGTM 004 (yield on treasury),
  GTC utility/empowerment threads.
* 2025 Delegate Nominations — governance body explicitly reconstituted.
* “3 Core Challenges to Solve in 2025” — public problem statement.

2026 — Post-Reset, Lean Era (AAA Tripod)

Low forum volume so far (28 topics) but high-signal: this is the executing year, not the debating
year.

* “Gitcoin 2026 Strategy — TL;DR” (MathildaDV, crediting Owocki): \~$20M treasury+matching, 4–7yr runway, framed as AAA Tripod — Alignment / Alpha / Accelerate. Explicit “no org debt, lean core team.”
* 2026 Budget passed.
* d/acc 2026 Funding Initiative — Grants program restructured around d/acc framing.
* Octant yield-powered matching pilot passed.
* Avantgarde disengaged from both treasury and matching-pool asset management.
* GG24 = Deep Funding (web3 tooling/infra round, predictive models, Verdict juror tooling).
* Akita × Gitcoin: Positive Path Forward — closing a 2021-era loop.
* “Consider Migrating Gitcoin DAO Governance to Degov” — even the forum/governance stack itself is up for replacement.

---

The arc, in one line per era:
2021 build the DAO → 2022 scale the DAO → 2023 decentralize via protocol → 2024 contract & focus → 2025 publicly re-found → 2026 execute lean, retro funding, close old loops.

The throughline: every \~2 years the forum hosts a paradigm-resetting megathread (Stewards Governance '21 → Grants 2.0 '22 → Decentralizing Grants '23 → End Game '24 → Gitcoin 3.0 '25 → 2026 Strategy TL;DR), each one \~2× shorter-lived than the last as the org gets leaner.

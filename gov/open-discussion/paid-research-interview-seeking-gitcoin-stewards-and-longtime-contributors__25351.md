# PAID Research Interview: Seeking Gitcoin stewards and longtime contributors

<https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/paid-research-interview-seeking-gitcoin-stewards-and-longtime-contributors/25351>
sela | 2026-07-24 22:54:13 UTC | #1

Hi Gitcoin community! I’m an innovation strategist at Jump Associates. We’re studying how member-led organizations cultivate stewardship and give members genuine ownership.

We’re looking for firsthand stories that bring Gitcoin to life: people becoming contributors and stewards, shaping decisions, navigating highs and lows, and building shared purpose, identity, and belonging. We’re also interested in what Gitcoin’s governance experiments and the broader DAO ecosystem have taught you.

We’d love to speak with a current or former core team member or a deeply involved Citizen, steward, delegate, council member, or contributor.

The virtual interview is about 60 minutes. We offer a **$400** honorarium, paid to you, a charity, or of course Gitcoin.

Please reply here or message me on [LinkedIn](https://shorturl.at/6uGOL)  if you’re interested or know someone we should contact. Thank you!

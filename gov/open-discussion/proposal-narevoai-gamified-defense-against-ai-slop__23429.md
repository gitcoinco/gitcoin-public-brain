# (Proposal) NarevoAI Gamified Defense Against AI Slop

<https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/proposal-narevoai-gamified-defense-against-ai-slop/23429>
NarevoAI | 2025-08-30 01:48:59 UTC | #1

**Project Description**

NarevoAI is a community-driven crypto project fighting AI slop low-quality, auto-generated content flooding the internet. We turn this into a game: users guess if content is AI or Human, earn points, and build a collective defense layer for the web.

Points later convert into tokens with governance + utility, rewarding those who keep the internet authentic.



**Value to the Web3 Ecosystem**

Strengthens trust: Builds tools for detecting AI-generated misinformation.

Community-first: Rewards crowdsourced intelligence instead of centralized AI filters.

Web3 alignment: Gamified engagement → onchain reputation + tokenized incentives.




**Use of Funds**

Gitcoin funding will help us:

1. Develop an MVP browser app (AI vs Human challenge).


2. Build a tokenized points system.


3. Onboard first 1,000 users through quests & community campaigns.


4. Research improved AI-detection models with open-source ethos.





**Long-Term Vision**

Narevo Nodes: Decentralized extensions for fact-checking + detection.

Governance DAO: Community votes on AI detection policies.

Integration: APIs for platforms to defend against AI slop.

Advisory support from Web3 ecosystem builders.

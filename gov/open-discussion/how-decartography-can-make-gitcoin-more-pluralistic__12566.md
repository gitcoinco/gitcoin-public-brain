**We are currently working on [DeCartography](https://decartography.github.io/docs/), a social diversity protocol to realize a "Plurality World".**

  DeCartography is one of the computational oracles that provide "multidimensional social diversity" between wallet addresses based on on-chain activity on Ethereum.

-  Developers using this oracle can use GraphQL to obtain the multidimensional relationship values between a given wallet address and other wallet addresses or groups (which are lists of wallet addresses).

  - The data used to generate the social graph is determined by the crowdworker. Crowdworkers are rewarded by solving commonsense problems displayed on the platform.

-   The goal is to collect ratings subjectively from each crowdworker and use the "wisdom of crowds" to provide a social graph that is as close to objective as possible.

Proof-of-work, one of the consensus algorithms employed in Bitcoin, etc., in which a large number of nodes allocate a specific cost to become validators to provide a specific function, is a representation (report, vote) based on the premise of 1Person, 1ID

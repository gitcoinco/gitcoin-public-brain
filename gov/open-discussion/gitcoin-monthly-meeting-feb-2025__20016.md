# Gitcoin Monthly Meeting - Feb 2025

<https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gitcoin-monthly-meeting-feb-2025/20016>
meglister | 2025-02-17 01:00:50 UTC | #1

At the start of each month, the Gitcoin team has an hour-long meeting that primarily focuses on strategy and and progress against our goals. This month, we focused on financial updates, sales pipeline review, and GG23.

Link: https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1-6PRhRlfnNCIMpzjbmX3I1volgfjBIDNXt6pRuu-rvM/edit#slide=id.g29a86529457_1_0

### **Summary**: 


1. **Kudos & Recognition** – Shoutouts to team members for their contributions.
2. **ETH Denver Update** – Sponsorships, logistics, and event schedule.
3. **2025 Funding & Goals** – Treasury status, funding strategy, and financial challenges.
4. **Grants Lab 2025** – $25M in grants with a goal of $125M in ecosystem impact.
5. **Revenue & Pipeline Updates** – Key revenue deals and targets.
6. **GG23 Update** – Upcoming Gitcoin Grants 2023 with various funding mechanisms.
7. **Q1 Rocks** – Strategic initiatives for Q1 2025.

---

### **Key Takeaways:**

#### **1. ETH Denver (Feb 23 - Mar 1)**

* **Sponsorships secured**: Stellar, Uniswap, Polygon, Filecoin, Metamask, and more.
* **Event Highlights**:
  * **Schelling Point (Feb 27)** – Major Gitcoin gathering.
  * **Allo Summoning Event (Feb 26)**
  * **Team Taco Tuesday (Feb 25)**
  * **Allo Chill & Shill (Mar 1)**

---

#### **2. Financial & Treasury Update**

* **Treasury decline due to token price drop**:
  * **Dec 2024**: $20M (GTC @ $1.10)
  * **Feb 2025**: ~$9.8M (GTC @ $0.42)
* **Cost reductions in place** but a focus on revenue is critical.
* **Spending Plans**:
  * GL H2 Funding: -$1.5M
  * Allo Funding 2025: -$2M
  * **EOY Treasury = $6.3M**

---

#### **3. Revenue Growth Strategy**

* **2025 Revenue Target**: $2M (Make-or-break goal)
* **Key Focus**:
  * Large deals & big customers.
  * Token utility experiments for GTC.
  * Optimizing non-stablecoin revenue.
* **January Revenue Performance**:
  * **Polygon deal**: $90K revenue.
  * **Filecoin fees**: $30K revenue.
  * **Total January Revenue**: $120K (12% of H1 goal).
* **Q1 Pipeline**:
  * Several smaller deals ($10K-$20K each).
  * A **large potential deal ($3M GMV @ 10% fee).**
  * **Total pipeline value: $397K.**

---

#### **4. Gitcoin Grants (GG23) – April 2-23**

* **Funding Rounds**:
  * **Quadratic Funding (QF)**:
    * $600K in matching.
    * Open to all Web3 projects.
  * **Retroactive Funding**:
    * Top 30 OSS projects from past Gitcoin Grants rounds.
    * $600K matching.
  * **Community Rounds**:
    * New governance model using Grant Ships.
    * Gamified incentives & on-chain success tracking.

---

#### **5. Q1 2025 Objectives ("Rocks")**

✅ **Multi-mechanism Gitcoin Grants Launch** – GG23 will feature new funding mechanisms. 
✅ **Builder Onboarding Initiative** – Target of 50 builders within 90 days. 
🟡 **$1M grant distribution via OGC** – In collaboration with OP Grants Council. 
🟡 **Enterprise Partnerships** – Ongoing discussions for a $3M+/year partnership.

---

### **Conclusion**

Gitcoin is facing **financial challenges** due to declining token prices but is focusing on **revenue growth, partnerships, and token utility experiments**. GG23 will introduce **new funding mechanisms**, and ETH Denver will be a key event for networking and ecosystem expansion. **Q1 2025 goals prioritize financial sustainability, community engagement, and product innovation.** 🚀

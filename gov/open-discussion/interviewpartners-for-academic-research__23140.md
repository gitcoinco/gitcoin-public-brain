# Interviewpartners for academic research

<https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/interviewpartners-for-academic-research/23140>
Rudolph | 2025-08-19 14:00:02 UTC | #1

Hey GitCoin-Community,

My name is Rudolph, and I am a student at the University of Passau in Germany. For my thesis, I am researching what makes DAOs sustainably successful. I am especially interested in understanding, from contributors’ perspectives, why people choose to participate in a DAO, how they find their place within it, and how their motivation shapes long-term engagement.

That’s why I am currently looking for DAO contributors who would be open to sharing their personal experiences and perspectives in a short, anonymous interview. Since GitCoin is such a vibrant community, I thought this would be a great place to find people who might be open to participating.

If you are interested in sharing your experiences, please feel free to reach out, DM me or reply to this message—I would greatly appreciate it!

Gm Gitcoin community, meet Public Good Africa - PGA!

It’s Grant season, we will be brief; we promise🤞🏾

# Overview

We are excited to announce PGA to this lovely community. PGA is a deliberate effort to ignite a new era of public good in Africa. We are creating a mechanism where funding, collaboration and impact converge to enable web3 to meet one of its noblest potential - empowering Africa!

# Specification

We are building in two folds 👷🏾‍♂️🏗️

* A platform to aggregate impact across Africa and integrate Gitcoin technologies to run QF rounds. This will enable anyone to discover impacts across the continent easily. It’s also a great way of connecting these impacts to various Public Good Funding opportunities.

* A coordination mechanism(community, education etc) to raise Africa’s collective consciousness about the power of Public Goods and Public Goods Funding in fostering innovation, impact and creating positive externalities.

Exciting Update 📰

* We have been seeded with 5k USD from Owocki to run a QF round for Public Goods in Africa. This will be the first of its kind. We are extremely grateful, thank you @owocki 
* We have been communicating with @jon-spark-eco since the launch of PGA, who has been an absolute public great in guiding us through a series of important steps.
* We have synced with the Quadratic Avengers 🫡🫡

We are excited for the future of Public Goods in Africa and the world.

And, that’s it, back to impacting.😉

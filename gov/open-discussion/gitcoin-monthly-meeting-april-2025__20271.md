# Gitcoin Monthly Meeting - April 2025

<https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gitcoin-monthly-meeting-april-2025/20271>
meglister | 2025-04-11 14:10:01 UTC | #1

At the start of each month, the Gitcoin team has an hour-long meeting that primarily focuses on strategy and and progress against our goals. This month, we focused on financial updates, product roadmap, sales pipeline review, and GG23.

Link: https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1JmpINXcgQCYHw3Ah7Nt47mISPfahrtwEq5hCdBPxgDI/edit#slide=id.g29a86529457_1_0 

---

### 🔥 **Key Agenda Items**

* Kudos
* Q1 Goals Update
* Pipeline & Revenue
* Product Roadmap
* Gitcoin Grants (GG23) Update
* Performance Reviews (starting April 16)

---

### 🙌 **Kudos**

@MathildaDV was recognized for outstanding leadership running **GG23**, managing multiple complex components: QF (Quadratic Funding), Retrofunding, and Staking.

---

### ✅ **Q1 Goals Wrap-Up**

**Accomplished:**

* ✅ Launch of **multi-mechanism Gitcoin Grants** with 3+ funding mechanisms.
* ✅ Publication of impact analysis planned.
* ✅ **Builder Onboarding program** launched.
* ✅ **$1.2M distributed** through the grants claiming tool.
* ✅ **X committed** $3M (major enterprise partner landed).

**Pending:**

* ❌ **Incentive Program** delayed post-GG23 due to resourcing.

---

### 📊 **Financial & Pipeline Overview**

#### **Q1 Performance**

* Revenue Progress: 50% of Q1 goal
* Total Revenue: **$201K**
* Total Cost: **$994K**
* Net Profit YTD: **- $700K**

#### **March Revenue Breakdown**

* Major boost from **Schelling Point sponsorships ($92.5K)**
* Revenue decline attributed to **token value drops**

#### **Notable Pipeline Updates**

* ✅ Closed a **3M GMV deal** (~$175K USDC fee)
* Promising Q2 leads:
  * Several deals in the **$1M–$5M GMV range**
  * A possible deal with the **International Olympic Committee**

---

### 🧭 **Product Roadmap Priorities**

* **Focus:** Revenue-driving features for enterprise customers
* **Audiences:**
  * Primary: Enterprise orgs (e.g., Sov, Quarksig)
  * Secondary: GG managers, builders, and donors
* **Prioritization Criteria:**
  * Business alignment
  * Ecosystem impact
  * Technical feasibility

---

### 🚀 **GG23 Updates**

* **Duration:** April 2–16 & April 9–23
* **Scale:** $1.3M+ matching funds
* **Experiments:**
  * 5 funding mechanisms
  * 10 community rounds
  * Predictive funding challenge
  * Deep governance
  * GTC staking

#### **Retro Round**

* Live voting by badgeholders, donors, and GTC holders

#### **Performance KPIs**

* $40K+ donations to Gitcoin
* Retrofunding leaderboard live

---

### 🛠 **Guilds Update**

* Engagement across **builders, grantees, donors**
* Initiatives launched:
  * Guild calls, Twitter spaces
  * Builder activations
  * Coordination tools
  * Data integration with GG23

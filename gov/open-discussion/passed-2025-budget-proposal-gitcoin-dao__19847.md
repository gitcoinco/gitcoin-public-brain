## **TL;DR**

This seeks funding for DAO operations through 2025 (January - December), including the Gitcoin Grants Program operations. The DAO 2025 Strategy presents an integrated approach to governance, builder engagement, and ecosystem growth, focusing on progressive decentralization and global expansion. We are requesting $808,098 from the treasury ($673,415 + 20% reserves) to execute this vision.

*Note: The Ecosystem Collective (formerly driving DAO operations and budget) dissolved mid-2024; this request represents our evolved strategic direction.*

## Budget Request

The DAO remains focused on stewarding Gitcoin’s brand, community and governance in support of the overall growth and health of the Gitcoin ecosystem.

For 2025, Gitcoin DAO is requesting $808,098 from the treasury for the year of 2025. No change since the last request, as this is the first request since Ecosystem Collective was dissolved mid 2024. After accounting for remaining budget & reserves, the DAO is requesting $808,098 for the 12 month period ($673,415.00 + 20%).

We will post updates and progress on the forum every quarter to ensure that the DAO is staying on track with accountability and progress throughout the year.

## **Key Goals for 2025**


### ***Governance***



* Updates to Governance Handbook: Define core principles and decision-making frameworks to guide progressive decentralization.
* Introduce a DAO Coordinator to oversee governance, context-sharing, and operational alignment.
* Attract and support builders through improved governance and community engagement.
* Decentralize governance processes, with 50%+ community-driven decisions by year-end.
* Onboard high-caliber stewards while fostering stronger DAO-to-DAO relationships.
* Execute a 2025 events strategy targeting APAC, SEA, and LATAM to expand builder engagement.
* Execute a new governance strategy that includes engaging 5 high-context ecosystem-savvy paid delegates.


### ***Builder Engagement***

By engaging and supporting builders, we’re directly increasing adoption and activity on Allo and within our GG rounds to support Gitcoin’s North Star.



* Expand Builder Community: Onboard 500+ new builders globally, with a focus on LATAM, APAC, and SEA.
* Promote Accessible Funding: Encourage 100+ Gitcoin Community Proposals (GCPs) and Requests for Proposals (RFPs).
* Support Innovation: Enable MVP development through hackathons and bounties to showcase Allo's potential.


### ***Regional Events***



* Host 3+ Regional Events: Engage builders in LATAM, APAC, and SEA through hackathons, workshops, and community meetups.
    * *As we won’t run events within the DAO budget directly, we’ll be requesting funds for events after completing research on high value areas to target and event planning.*
* Amplify Ecosystem Awareness: Ensure 70% of attendees understand Gitcoin’s broader ecosystem.
* Promote Local Innovation: Partner with local organizations to co-create impactful funding experiments.

## Program Integration & Support

The DAO budget directly supports the execution of the [Gitcoin Grants 2025 Strategy](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gitcoin-grants-2025-proposed-strategy/19723), particularly:

- Operational support for three major OSS rounds
- Resources for the multi-mechanism pilot programs
- Infrastructure for metrics-based funding implementation
- Support for the new Grant Ships governance vehicle
- Community council transition in Q1 2025

Through the DAO Coordinator and Grants Program roles, this budget ensures proper execution of the program's key objectives:

- 50% Builder retention target
- $4.29M+ distribution across rounds
- Community council evolution
- Weekly builder demos
- Enhanced governance mechanisms

Additional program details can be found in the [full Grants Program strategy](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gitcoin-grants-2025-proposed-strategy/19723).

# [PROPOSAL] Budget Request - Schelling Point & Eth Denver 2025

jehanav | 2025-02-02 17:50:57 UTC | #1

**TL:DR**
We are requesting **$78,994** (USD equivalent in GTC) to fund activations for the 2025 Gitcoin event activations at Eth Denver (including our [Schelling Point](https://lu.ma/qr9mbaqz) event) from the Gitcoin Treasury in line with our 2025 [Grants Lab](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gitcoin-2025-planning-recap/19769), and [Gitcoin Grants](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gitcoin-grants-2025-proposed-strategy/19723) Strategies. The overall costs have been offset by shared payments with Allo and sponsorships for the event. 

**Introduction**

We’re excited to announce our plans for ETHDenver 2025—our biggest event of the year and a pivotal opportunity to solidify Gitcoin’s brand narrative and market positioning. This proposal outlines the event overview, a detailed breakdown of planned activations, and the associated budget, including allocations for merchandise and travel.

**Goals at ETHDenver:**

* **Reinforce Gitcoin’s Evolution:** Highlight our transition toward providing cutting-edge grants technology and services for large ecosystems.
* **Establish Industry Leadership:** Position Gitcoin as the go-to leader in grants management and decentralized funding.
* **Grow Conviction Among Builders:** Demonstrate the power of Allo to developers through hands-on activations that drive conviction in our tools and onboard new builders.
* **Strengthen Strategic Patnerships:** Deepen relationships with key partners and showcase impactful collaborations that spotlight Gitcoin's contributions.

**Gitcoin at ETH Denver (2/23/25-3/2/25)**

**Activations**

Below are our scheduled events for the week:

Sunday, Feb 23 to Tuesday, Feb 25:** Arrivals and setup, Gitcoin Contributor team check-in, preparation, and in-person collaboration
* Wednesday, Feb 26: Allo Summoning Event (venue booked)
* Thursday, Feb 27: Schelling Point at Number 38 (venue booked)
* Thursday, Feb 27: Gitcoin Grants Happy Hour (venue booked)
* Unscheduled: Grants F*ck Up Night (private evening event)
* Unscheduled: Additional partner sponsored events and activations

NOTE: We will be co-sponsoring Schelling Point and events at the Gitcoin + Allo House (including splitting costs) with Allo Capital.

**Schelling Point Overview**

Schelling Point is our flagship event and is evolving this year to reflect Gitcoin’s shifting focus. While public goods funding remains close to our hearts and the ongoing focus of Gitcoin Grants, the broader organizational focus has shifted towards enabling builders through a plurality of mechanisms and access to funding from large ecosystems.

Reflecting this shift, the Schelling Point theme has been established as “Scaling Builder Success: A Multi-Mechanism Future”. Through partnership in the form of sponsorships from forward-thinking ecosystems, we will showcase and collaborate on innovative approaches to enabling builder success. Programming will be co-created with our sponsors and speakers. Below are a few examples of the topics that might be included:

* **Growth across the builder lifecycle:** How to leverage a plurality of mechanisms to support builders across every step – from onboarding to retention and growth.
* **How verifiable impact drives sustainable growth:** Learn the latest about how top ecosystems are allocating capital with a focus on outcomes and ROI
* **Retro done right:** The evolution and impact of retro funding, and how to get it right.
* **Workshop with Gitcoin:** Assess your program with Gitcoin’s Grants Maturity Framework and gain actionable tips to evolve your program to the next level.
* **Mechanism Design:** What are the key elements of a successful mechanism - everything from allocation strategy to applicant and evaluator UX, and much more.
* **Access through design:** Exploring how to leverage mechanism design to drive greater financial inclusion

We are targeting an attendance of 400 people at the event, with participation from ecosystem leaders, grants program managers, key builders and Web3 thought leaders. We are prioritizing sponsorship from our top partners, with the opportunity to spotlight innovations in grants design that Gitcoin supported over the past year.

**Sponsorships**

We are currently issuing contracts for $62k in sponsorships and are negotiating an additional $35k to support Schelling Point and other relevant costs. We will deliver a comprehensive expense report following the event, accompanied by a recap of the week that includes ROI insights and key performance metrics. We are confident that event expenses will remain below budget and our cost estimates have been purposefully rounded up to ensure we have flexibility to handle any unexpected costs, which are typically the case with large-scale events. The team is dedicated to being responsible with spending, ensuring the DAO’s treasury is used efficiently and for essential, mission-driven needs. Any surplus funds from the activation budget or sponsorships will be returned to the Gitcoin treasury.

​Hi everyone, we are pleased to provide an update on Gitcoin's activations during ETHDenver 2025, focusing on our strategic events: Schelling Point, the Gitcoin Grants After Party, and Allo House. These initiatives were designed to enhance Gitcoin's leadership in the grants ecosystem and strengthen our community and partnerships.​

**TL;DR:**

* Gitcoin primarily funded ETHDenver 2025 events through Schelling Point sponsorships ($92,000), limiting spend to only $2,949, which has been absorbed as a Grants Lab expense.

* Schelling Point enhanced Gitcoin’s brand presence and leadership in grants but did not yield new partnerships—making partnership-building a key focus moving forward.

* Strong attendance for Schelling Point with over 1,100 registrations, ~500 in-person attendees, and 3,700 livestream viewers.

* Financial Overview

* In light of market volatility, we opted not to utilize the initially requested budget of $78,994 from Gitcoin's treasury. Instead, we successfully secured sponsorships totaling $92,000 from Schelling Point, which covered the majority of our expenses. The total expenditures amounted to $94,949, resulting in a net cost to Gitcoin of $2,949. We will be returning the previously requested GTC to the treasury.

**Expense Breakdown:**

* **Schelling Point:** $78,473​

* **Allo House:** $1,717​

* **Team Travel:** $14,759​

**Schelling Point Highlights**

Schelling Point emerged as a pivotal event for the Gitcoin brand. Our goals for the event were:​

* Establish Gitcoin’s leadership in grants.​

* Strengthen strategic partnerships.​

Key Outcomes:

* **Sponsorship Success:** Secured $92,000 in sponsorships, significantly offsetting event costs. Notable sponsors included Butter, Uniswap, Optimism (Diamond sponsors), Stellar, Polygon, Filecoin, Powerhouse, Holonym, Divvi, and OpenLedger. Collectively, these organizations have distributed over $50 million in grants funding over the past year.​

* **Engaging Programming:** Hosted two stages featuring industry leaders and collaborated with key partners such as Polygon, Filecoin, Butter, and Celo.​

* **Attendance and Reach:** Attracted over 1,100 registrations, with approximately 500 in-person attendees and 3,700 livestream viewers.​

* **Positive Feedback:** Received exceptional participant feedback, with many attendees citing Schelling Point as their favorite ETHDenver side event.​

* **Thematic Focus:** Emphasized enabling builder success through diverse mechanisms, showcasing Gitcoin's evolution toward offering comprehensive tools and services for ecosystem growth.​

Schelling Point was a huge success from a brand perspective. For the rest of the year, we will shift focus for our events strategy on new partnerships, aligning with our commitment to financial sustainability and strategic resource allocation.

**Gitcoin Grants After Party**

Following the Grants session at Schelling Point, we hosted the Gitcoin Grants After Party at the Church House. This event provided an intimate setting for the Grants team to connect with builders. Out of 50 RSVPs, approximately 25-30 builders attended, engaging in meaningful conversations and community bonding around the fire pit and within the venue.​

**Allo House**

Allo House served as a collaborative space for the team and partners throughout ETHDenver, facilitating various discussions and networking opportunities. The expenditure of $1,717 contributed to creating an environment conducive to fostering connections and sharing ideas among attendees.​

In summary, Gitcoin's activations at ETHDenver 2025 successfully met our strategic objectives, enhancing our position in the grants ecosystem and fostering valuable community and partner relationships. The prudent financial management and overwhelming support from our sponsors were instrumental in achieving these outcomes.

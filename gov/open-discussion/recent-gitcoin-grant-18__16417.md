It's also worth mentioning that any donations from GG18 will not be added to the GrantsStack Passport stamp right away.  There is a note that mentions it may not be until the end of September.  The stamps should also have a note saying that any donations require matching to be eligible for the stamp, and when checking out your cart should show the minimum amount required for matching.  If you were able to pick some projects that you wanted to support, I would not consider it a "waste".  Sorry for the confusion during the process.

# My 30/60/90 Plan as Executive Steward

# Strategic Direction

My goal as Executive Steward is to return Gitcoin to its former glory—socially, economically, and reputationally. To achieve this, I’ll anchor our efforts around three priorities:

1. Fund and help solve Ethereum’s biggest problems  
2. Evolve best-in-class capital allocation software  
3. Achieve “3,3,3,3” alignment across stakeholders — builders, domain providers, funders, and Gitcoin — so cooperation compounds for everyone.  
   - **What “3,3,3,3” Means (Quick Note)** \- Shorthand for mutually reinforcing positive sum games among four stakeholder groups: Ethereum, domain providers, funders, and Gitcoin. If each plays positive-sum games with the others, the flywheel spins faster for everyone.

## **What is a 30/60/90 Plan?**

A 30/60/90 plan is a structured roadmap that outlines priorities and expected outcomes for the first three months of a new role. It breaks down work into three phases—establishing foundations in the first 30 days, executing and iterating in the next 60, and delivering outcomes and setting longer-term direction by 90\. The goal is to provide clarity, accountability, and momentum from day one.

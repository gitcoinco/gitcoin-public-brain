Hi everyone,

I’m finishing my thesis at HEC Paris on the "Protocol-Governed Innovation System (PGIS)" — a blockchain-enabled, multi-level transformation of innovation that starts with business model innovations, scales up to new archetypes of innovation ecosystems, and, when combined and underpinned by Ethereum, forms a new system. My research examines how this transformation in the innovation environment redefines the nature and limits of the firm. Gitcoin is a key infrastructure provider within this framework, enabling funding, coordination, and collaboration that help drive these innovation ecosystems. 

I’m looking to speak with contributors, grantees, or governance participants to hear your experiences and perspectives. A 20–45 minute interview via Zoom, Discord, Teams, or Google Meet would be fantastic — but even answering a short set of questions in your own time over Telegram would be a huge help.

If you’re interested, please drop a comment or DM me — I’d be truly grateful for your time and insights.

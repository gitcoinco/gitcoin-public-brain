Hey everyone heads up that the first [Moonshot Collective Workstream](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/workstream-dapp-prototyping-the-moonshot-collective/130/92)  call is tomorrow at 12pm MST. Sign up at https://moonshotcollective.space/ to get the calendar invite :slight_smile:

For more details on the what/why behind this checkout https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/workstream-dapp-prototyping-the-moonshot-collective/130  or check this out below:


> 📣 We are the GitcoinDAO rapid prototyping workstream. Our deep reason for being is to accelerate innovation by
> - supporting rapid prototyping in the space 🛠
> - help builders build moonshots 🚀
> - to create fellowship for web3 builders 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
> - to HAVE fun while doing it. 🃏
>
> 🖖 Agenda:
> 1. G&A (5-10 min)
> 2. Moonshot Pitches (40 mins)
> 3. Moonshart Celebrations (10 min)
> 4. Chat, Chillin', Shillin
>
> 👩‍🚀 Prep Work:
> 1. Please enter your moonshot pitch into https://sli.do (code MOONSHOT) prior to the call.
> 2. Got any moonSHARTS (failed projects)? Come prepared to share the story of how they failed & what you learned!

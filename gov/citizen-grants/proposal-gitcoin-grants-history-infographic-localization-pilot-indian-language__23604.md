# 🌍 Proposal: Gitcoin Grants History Infographic + Localization Pilot (Indian Language)

<https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/proposal-gitcoin-grants-history-infographic-localization-pilot-indian-language/23604>
manas_1991 | 2025-09-06 09:29:59 UTC | #1

Hi everyone,

This proposal suggests creating a visual infographic of Gitcoin Grants history — showing the messy, beautiful, evolutionary journey of the program. To make it accessible globally, we will also run a localization pilot by translating the infographic into Hindi and Odia, engaging new communities in India and testing how visuals + local languages can improve onboarding.

Gitcoin has a long and evolving history, but new Citizens often find it overwhelming to understand past Grants Rounds.

A visual storytelling tool will:

Simplify onboarding for Citizens and grantees.

Celebrate Gitcoin’s evolution as a community.

Provide a shareable resource for education and outreach.


Adding localization ensures inclusivity: many potential contributors outside English-speaking regions find governance inaccessible.

Scope & Deliverables

1. Infographic Poster → Gitcoin Grants journey (GG1 → GG24).


2. Localized Versions → Hindi + Odia translation.


3. Community Release → Published openly on Gitcoin forum + Discord, downloadable by anyone.


4. Optional Print-ready Version → usable in hackathons, meetups, and onboarding sessions.

Budget Request

Infographic Design (data viz freelancer) → $800

Translation & Review (Hindi + Odia) → $400

Coordination, publishing, community feedback → $300


Total: $1,500 (paid in USDC/GTC)

KPIs (Success Metrics)

Infographic completed in 3 languages (English, Hindi, Odia).

At least 200 views/downloads in the first month.

At least 10 new Citizens onboarded who found Gitcoin easier to understand through this resource.

Positive feedback: 70%+ of surveyed users rate it useful for onboarding.

Impact for Gitcoin

Improves onboarding & inclusivity.

Creates a reusable template for other DAOs (Gitcoin leading by example).

Strengthens Gitcoin’s identity as a global public goods platform.

Demonstrates how visual + localized tools can bridge governance gaps.


By combining data visualization and localization, this pilot strengthens Gitcoin’s global onboarding, reduces barriers for non-English speakers, and showcases Gitcoin’s story in a way that is engaging and accessible.

— Proposed by Manas (Citizen of Gitcoin, Governance & Policy Contributor)

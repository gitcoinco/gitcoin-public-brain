### Proposal Title:

Creating Educational Content and Growing Community for the Gitcoin Ecosystem in Spanish

### Proposal Description:

This proposal aims to develop high-quality educational content to increase awareness, engagement, and participation in the Gitcoin ecosystem among the Spanish-speaking community. Through tutorial videos, expert interviews, newsletters, and social media content, we will provide accessible resources to help users navigate Gitcoin's tools, funding mechanisms, and its mission in public goods funding.

### Interested Parties and Expected Impact:

* Gitcoin Community and Developers: Increased adoption and usage of Gitcoin grants, quadratic funding, and decentralized governance tools.
* Spanish-Speaking Audience: Greater understanding of the Gitcoin ecosystem, leading to increased participation in funding rounds and governance.
* Gitcoin Network Effect: A more educated user base contributing to Gitcoin's mission and strengthening the decentralized funding movement.
* Gitcoin Community Support: Telegram users such as @MathildaDV and @wasabinetwork support this proposal, even offering to participate in the podcasts.

### Motivation:

Gitcoin is revolutionizing public goods funding through quadratic funding and open-source development, but Spanish-speaking users lack structured educational content to fully understand and participate in the ecosystem. By creating high-impact content, we aim to:

* Educate users about Gitcoin grants, funding processes, and governance.
* Encourage adoption by breaking down complex concepts into easy-to-understand content.
* Expand the Gitcoin ecosystem in Latin America and Spain by fostering a committed community.

I have no conflicts of interest in this proposal. The Gitcoin Citizen Grants Program should adopt this proposal because education is key to scaling Gitcoin’s mission globally. Without clear, structured educational materials, many developers and potential contributors may not fully understand how to leverage Gitcoin’s tools.

### Specifications:

#### Scope of Activities:

* Production of 8 high-quality tutorial videos explaining Gitcoin grants, how to participate in funding rounds, and governance mechanisms.
* Creation of 6 detailed educational videos analyzing the impact of Gitcoin funding rounds, community-led projects, and the evolution of decentralized public goods funding.
* Podcast Series (3 expert interviews) featuring Gitcoin contributors and leaders in public goods funding.
* Production of 17 short videos for social media (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts) summarizing key Gitcoin concepts to drive participation.
* Publication of 3 educational newsletters with updates, information, and success stories within the Gitcoin ecosystem.

#### Technical Details:

* Video Content: Professionally edited and optimized for YouTube and social media.
* Podcasts: Distributed via YouTube, Spotify, and Gitcoin community channels.
* Newsletters: Hosted on LaVidaCrypto.com and shared with Gitcoin’s community mailing lists.
* Tracking Metrics: Analytical tools will be implemented to measure reach, engagement, and conversions from the educational content.

* 3 podcast episodes featuring interviews with Gitcoin experts.
* 17 short social media videos to encourage participation.

### 🎯 **Measurable Objectives**

#### **Increase the participation of Hispanic projects in Gitcoin Grants:**

* **Goal:** Increase by **30%** the number of Spanish-speaking projects applying to the rounds.
* **Action:** Creation of detailed tutorial videos explaining the application process, best practices, and strategies to obtain funding.

#### **Improve the visibility and success of Spanish-speaking projects within the rounds:**

* **Goal:** Ensure that at least **5 Hispanic projects** stand out in the highest fundraising category within their respective rounds.
* **Action:** Promotion through strategic content, interviews with founders, and live project analysis.

#### **Facilitate onboarding of users and creators in Gitcoin Grants:**

* **Goal:** Achieve at least **1,000 views** on educational tutorials during the Grant period.
* **Action:** Creation of content optimized for positioning on YouTube.

#### **Generate impact reports and measure results:**

* **Goal:** Present a final report with data on conversions, content reach, and participation rates of Spanish-speaking users.
* **Action:** Implementation of **UTMs in shared links** to measure traffic and adoption.

---

### 📆 **Execution Plan and Milestones**

#### **Milestone 1: Creation of Educational Content and Distribution Strategy**

* **Date:** Month 1
* **Objective:** Establish the content strategy framework and produce the first educational materials.
* **Deliverables:**
  * **3 educational videos:** Explanation of Gitcoin Grants and quadratic funding.
  * **1 blog/tutorial article** detailing how to apply and optimize a campaign on Gitcoin Grants.
  * **Distribution strategy design** (Twitter, Telegram, YouTube).

#### **Milestone 2: Promotion and Onboarding of Projects and Donors**

* **Date:** Months 2 and 3
* **Objective:** Drive active participation of the community in Gitcoin Grants.
* **Deliverables:**
  * **3 video interviews** with Hispanic project founders who have received funding.
  * **4 educational videos** on best practices to maximize donations in the rounds.
  * **2 live YouTube sessions** with Q&A discussions.
  * **1 downloadable guide** for applying to Gitcoin Grants.

#### **Milestone 3: Impact Evaluation and Optimization**

* **Date:** Month 4
* **Objective:** Measure the initiative's results and prepare a continuity plan.
* **Deliverables:**
  * **Conversion and engagement metrics report.**
  * **Collection of testimonials** from funded projects.
  * **Continuity plan** based on results obtained.

---

### 📊 **Impact Measurement Strategy**

To ensure that the content has a real impact, we will implement:

* **Trackable links (UTMs)** in each educational material to measure conversions to Gitcoin Grants.
* **Monitoring the growth of the Hispanic community** in Gitcoin Grants in terms of new projects and donors.
* **User feedback surveys** for participants in the rounds after watching the content.
* **Comparison with previous Hispanic participation metrics** in Gitcoin Grants to evaluate the impact.

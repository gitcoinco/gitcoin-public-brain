## Proposal Description

### Short Summary

Gardens is a community governance platform optimized for bottom-up decision-making. It uses an enforceable Covenant tied to modular conviction voting pools to securely and intelligently signal sentiment and allocate resources to communities and ecosystems as effectively as possible.

This proposal seeks **$32,000** to enhance Gardens' UI/UX and implement key improvements needed to transition Gardens from a niche product to one capable of mass adoption.

**Amendment:** In addition, Gardens will allocate to Gitcoin 5,000 ⚘GARDEN plus an ongoing 2.5% of future governance token issuance going forward.


### Impacted Stakeholders & Expected Outcome

* **GG24 Community Participants** - Enhanced conviction voting experience with streamlined workflows and a friendlier UX
* **Gardens Communities** - Improved platform usability and mobile accessibility
* **Allo Protocol Ecosystem** - Increased TVF by scaling adoption of funding pools on Gardens
* **Gitcoin Stewards** - Professional-grade decision-making infrastructure for ecosystem growth and community coordination

**Expected Outcomes:** 2x increase in Gardens usage, significant reduction in onboarding friction, and 5x increase in TVF through Allo by EOY 2025.

### Who We Are

We are a team of active builders and maintainers of the [Gardens platform](https://app.gardens.fund/). Gardens is a key ecosystem partner of Gitcoin, having run 2 successful community rounds to date.

In GG22, our Gardens Community Round allocated $30k to communities and raised $14k in external funding - a 1.7x multiplier on Gitcoin's $20k investment ([retrospective](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gg22-community-round-proposal-growing-the-public-gardens/19397/8)).

GG23 significantly improved on these results, with $58k allocated and $40k external funding raised - a 3.05x multiplier on Gitcoin's $21k investment ([retrospective](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gitcoin-grants-garden-gg23-retrospective/20720)).

We hope to continue this trajectory in our [GG24 Exit-to-Community round](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gg24-dda-exit-to-community/21951), and are expecting increased Gardens usage in other DDA + community rounds as part of [Gitcoin 3.0's multi-mechanism future](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gitcoin-3-0-network-first-funding-festival-for-ethereum-s-biggest-problems/20355).

## Motivation

Despite finding product-market fit with niche community builders in pop-up cities, regen communities, and ecosystem growth orgs, Gardens has flown under the radar due to user unfriendliness, complexity, and competitive landscape challenges. This funding directly addresses these shortcomings and brings Gardens from an experimental mechanism for advanced users to a platform ready for mass adoption.

This is our first funding proposal to the Gitcoin community. To date, Gardens has raised $120k for development of our v2 platform from other sources (see our [fundraising spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/13uHSFEazZ14Pf2aolaZaVNAqJFRLXCliVOAqtOy9PZY/edit?gid=1728375145#gid=1728375145)), and has had great engagement in the past year from Gitcoin’s stewards and its broader ecosystem.

### Token Allocation
To further align the Gardens and Gitcoin communities, Gardens will also distribute a token allocation to Gitcoin as part of this proposal. ⚘GARDEN is the governance token for Gardens and is issued through Juicebox: https://juicebox.money/v4/op:34 - it serves to distribute collective ownership and source decisions for the platform.

The allocation terms are as follows:
* 5,000 ⚘GARDEN transferred to a Gitcoin community address
* 2.5% ongoing allocation issued through the [community reserve on Juicebox](https://juicebox.money/v4/op:34?tabid=tokens) 

Any future changes to the ongoing token allocation must be executed by the [Gardineros Core Contributors Safe](https://app.gardens.fund/gardens/10/0x8b2f706cd2bc0df6679218177c56e72c5241de9b/0x59c47c30da2a0ca7359590f023da0284fef83e73/146). The Gardens team encourages Gitcoin to propose 1-2 members to join this Safe and be represented in platform governance moderation going forward.  

Gardens uses a [2-token model](https://paragraph.com/@paul2.eth/the-2-token-model-for-public-goods) to separate the functions of governance and value accrual for the platform in separate tokens. While the ⚘GARDEN token is not currently liquid, the community has defined its target financial sustainability goal [in Juicebox](https://juicebox.money/v4/op:34?tabid=about) at **100 ETH by May of 2026**. If reached, Gardens will enter its next phase of growth, which includes establishing liquidity for the governance token and launching an endowment token for value accrual.

## Core Proposal Benefits

Gardens is built on Allo Protocol, meaning funds allocated directly contribute to Gitcoin's Total Value Flowed (TVF) metrics. Funds from this proposal would directly tie to Gitcoin’s ecosystem growth by increasing the adoption of Gardens, leading to more Allo TVF and direct funding for the dozens of projects on Gardens aligned with Gitcoin’s objective to Fund What Matters.

With the $32k requested in this proposal, our deliverables would promote:

**Gardens Community Engagement**

* Streamlined UX reducing the barriers to adopt and participate on Gardens
* Educational workshops helping onboard communities in our key market verticals
* Mobile optimization allowing for broader global participation

**Improved Network Effects**

* Growing the adoption of a community governance platform with direct ties to Gitcoin
* Providing a better decision mechanism for other round operators in GG24
* Expanding on a proven track record of amplifying Gitcoin investment through external funding from Gardens other funding partners including 1Hive, CeloPG, and Public Nouns

**Ensuring Financial Sustainability**

* Creating a more future-proof foundation for Gitcoin 3.0’s multi-mechanism landscape.
